# Changelog

All notable changes to the official project are documented here. Entries follow
the Keep a Changelog structure and Semantic Versioning (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`).

Earlier development drafts, experiments and organizational mockups were never official releases. They remain accessible through Git history.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Office of the COO charter: scoped Integration decisions, CoS coordination and conditional capacity/planning work, without intermediate Head reporting.
- Owner-preauthorized, qualified, consenting acting-COO procedure and explicit Council chair/seat continuity; senior initial-adjudicator pool proposal retaining independent case separation.
- Accessible pre-cutover authority reference, coherent R14 ratification/cutover package, fixed 20/30/60 rosters, conserved workload/backlog models and comparative adversarial tests.
- Current semantic-reference, delegation, succession, case, workload and negative validation checks.

### Fixed

- Restored executable six-stage managerial capability review, meaningful role profiles, worked examples and templates behind legacy anchors.
- Reconciled obsolete active portfolio escalation routes and updated task/website navigation, rank appointments and technical/incident interfaces.

Documentation specification only: R14 supersedes conflicting unapproved targets; no ratification, actual staffing, reserve capacity, live security tests or measured performance improvement is claimed. Existing license and all prior official releases remain unchanged.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-02

### Changed

- Selected three divisions plus compact Technology Office; removed permanent Executive Directors and Unit Manager layer from the target.
- Reassigned all original functions, revised ranks/appointments, authority, succession and staffing, and reduced intra-division home-transfer approval to one Head.
- Proposed an elected five-seat Council independent of optional executive offices, with formal ratification and fair personnel cutover gates.
- Added complete 20/30/60 staffing models, 60-person fictional roster, paired stress tests and structural negative checks.

Target specification only: R13 and outstanding case/operational powers require actual adoption. No appointments, controls or improved performance are claimed; prior history and LICENSE retained.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Three staff-facing guides: role-based quick starts, 16 everyday tasks, and reporting/appeal help, with links to controlling policies.
- Compact role profiles alongside all directorate, executive and protected oversight mandates.
- Analytical newcomer walkthroughs, 20/30/60-person simplicity tests and explicit before/after decision, approval, handoff and reading-path comparisons.

### Changed

- Task-based Starlight navigation, direct landing-page task links and collapsed reference groups; no duplicate policy sources or new dependencies.
- Clarified the distinction between line reporting and approval, existing-record reuse, scheduled checks, optional feedback, emergency notices and protected case records.
- Separated the publication allowlist from sidebar configuration and added checks for v1.1 policy preservation, source reachability and repository-only exclusions.

These are editorial, navigational and instructional improvements under existing authority. No approval requirement, rank eligibility, case power, recurring review or volunteer safeguard is removed. GOV-01–04 remain pending; no operational adoption, user-research result or real-world speed/retention improvement is claimed. LICENSE and earlier release entries remain unchanged.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Operational Readiness & Governance Improvements documentation specification: complete case lifecycle, disciplinary authority matrix, proportionate sanctions, independent appeals, qualified alternates and external-review limits.
- Incident activation, domain-limited command, handover, stand-down, platform-safety reporting and post-incident review procedures.
- Temporary authority, succession, volunteer capacity/service reductions, management capability review and functional promotion assessment.
- Executable access approval/revocation, bot containment/recovery, evidence custody and proposed retention/deletion schedules.
- Explicit ratification package, approval/implementation register and a second 15-scenario tabletop with remaining blockers.

### Changed

- Specified a proposed elected interim five-seat Council and recusal substitutes without changing the existing charter before required adoption.
- Replaced conflicting launch/Executive Director activation guidance with approval-gated interim governance and workload-based portfolio activation.
- Consolidated reporting around one action/exception register; preserved eight grades, six directorates, protected oversight functions, assignment boundaries and volunteer safeguards.
- Updated navigation, website specification notices, release metadata and exact authorized-amendment preservation checks.

This is a released documentation specification. Council votes, Owner ratification, officer appointments, external reserve availability and live technical controls are **not verified**. Proposed powers require their stated approvals; tabletop results are fictional, not operational measurements. LICENSE is unchanged.
