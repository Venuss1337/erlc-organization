# Changelog

All notable changes to the official project are documented here. Entries follow
the Keep a Changelog structure and Semantic Versioning (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`).

Earlier development drafts, experiments and organizational mockups were never official releases. They remain accessible through Git history.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Formalized organizational governance, executive leadership and the Governance Council.
- Eight-level organizational rank hierarchy and six operating directorates.
- Independent oversight with Internal Affairs, Appeals & Member Advocacy, and Standards & Assurance.
- Personnel, recruitment, cross-team assignments, appointments and career progression frameworks.
- Operational authority, Discord permissions, escalation and accountability procedures.
- Modular documentation architecture with canonical policy ownership, navigation, examples, templates and validation.
