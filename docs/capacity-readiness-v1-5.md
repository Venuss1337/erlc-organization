# v1.5 management capacity and oversight resilience evidence

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Repository-only engineering/tabletop evidence, not adopted policy or operational proof.** All identities/qualifications/windows/hours below are fictional scenario inputs. Actual reserve authorization, acceptance and access remain false. The independent v1.4 audit in the conversation supplies the input; no standalone audit file or signatures are invented. Canonical policy is the capacity charter and QORP, not this report.

## Baseline and modeling method

Compared immutable v1.4 commit 0768f8f700ef59fa997b2b525d2d7e8988fd60ed with R15. Earlier implementation data covered the executive queue; v1.5 explicitly adds nine whole-role categories for EVERY person: delivery, permanent coaching, functional tasks, executive Office, casework, training, administration, incidents and necessary coordination. The old executive JSON remains historical scenario data, not an extra workload to add on top of these complete-person vectors. Before busy Head demand uses the audit 4.3/5.6/4.9/4.2h examples; normal = .7 times busy, shortage = 1.1 times busy, and incident adds 1.5h to COO, Safety/Game/Technical Heads, S04 and IA. These factors are assumptions, not empirical demand forecasts. Generic contributor delivery is 3h plus .4h administration/training; 6h availability is not 6h compulsory work.

Six source-to-recipient functional transfers move 3.4h of routine Head execution. Each functional recipient adds .1h necessary coordination: .6h total extra work. Each actual People Lead releases 1.5h of optional frontline service (explicitly reduced output, not a time saving). Reserve casework replaces the SAME person’s delivery hours; it is not added free capacity. New protected case work and displaced delivery are separately visible. No additional permanent supervisor or person is added.
