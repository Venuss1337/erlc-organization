# Qualified Oversight Reserve Pool

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R15-O + retained GOV-02 proposal — Pending Required Approval.** QORP defines capability and case appointments under protected OIO jurisdiction, NOT a fourth operating division, another Council or a new supervisory chain. No actual qualified reserve, consent, appointment, safe storage or readiness is verified. [R15 adoption](../proposals/v1-5-ratification.md) controls activation.

## Separate capabilities and accountable owners

| Category | Required assessed competence | Work permitted after case assignment | Prohibited combination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reserve Investigator | Evidence handling/integrity, impartial fact-finding, interviewing, confidentiality, conflict screening and fair response | Scoped fact inquiry and reasoned findings | Own initial adjudication, own appeal or assurance of own inquiry |
| Reserve Appeals Reviewer | Relevant policy, procedural review, evidence evaluation, reasoned remedies, independence/conflicts and confidentiality | Independent eligible appeal within approved mandate | Reviewing own action, prior advice/investigation or adjudication |
| Reserve Standards & Assurance Assessor | Process/policy comprehension, audit/review skill, sensitive-information handling and independence from activity | Scoped systems assurance and recommendations | Auditing own implementation, own case decisions or own Office administration |

Initial adjudicators remain in the SEPARATE [senior adjudicator pool](case-handling.md#qualified-initial-adjudicator-pool). QORP membership does not qualify or appoint an adjudicator. Multiple individually assessed capabilities are allowed, but no incompatible roles in a case or linked decision question. Commissioner administers capacity/assignments without determining merits or receiving blanket files. Protected leads retain professional discretion.

## Eligibility, assessment and reserve registration

Existing staff may apply; another division alone does not establish independence. Former staff need current assessment, fresh confidentiality/access checks and no standing access from past service. External consenting candidates need equivalent relevant skill, identity/contact verification sufficient for safe work without intrusive personal data, conflicts and actual availability. No outsider is assumed available or receives server administration by participation.

1. Applicant/nomination names desired category and relevant evidence; self-nomination is allowed.
2. Unconflicted Commissioner checks eligibility with the relevant protected lead; if either is implicated, use the existing special external appointor. The applicant receives no real case files for selection.
3. Qualified unconflicted functional assessor uses a short redacted/synthetic work sample plus fairness/confidentiality questions against the category criteria. If no assessor exists, status remains candidate; a friend or automated test cannot certify expertise.
4. Record category-specific result, evidence, scope limits and conflicts. One passed category never supplies another qualification. Applicant accepts confidentiality, withdrawal rights and realistic windows.
5. Commissioner registers ordinary qualified reserves under adopted delegated power. Senior/OIO/Owner-related cases still use their special appointors; pool registration cannot authorize their own selection. Record valid authorizer, relevant assessment, consent and next review in the existing restricted capability register.
6. Registration lasts at most six months, with change-of-role/conflict review and proportionate refresher/synthetic sample on lapse or material policy change. No mandatory new certification ladder or rank.
7. Reserve can decline/withdraw without misconduct. Appointor can suspend eligibility for missing competence/access/confidentiality or unresolved conflict with recorded reasons and unconflicted functional advice; disagreement with a finding is not grounds. Notify affected case owners and provide an independent process challenge through existing alternate rules. Expiry ends eligibility/access, not a secret finding reversal.

Readiness stages are distinct: **defined → candidate identified → category assessed → registration authorized → specific availability accepted → case mandate/access verified**. No stage implies the next. Keep actual identities/windows/conflicts outside Git; publish only safe capacity limitations. Review changes and monthly open-case capacity using existing records, not another application bureaucracy.

## Case activation and independence

1. Intake identifies the protected question, urgency and category; separate linked ban appeal and abuse inquiry rather than duplicate investigation.
2. Unconflicted Commissioner/relevant lead screens primary availability and conflicts. If deficient, find category-qualified reserves; a subject never assigns their own reviewer.
3. Screen case-specific reporting dependence, subject's managerial influence, collaboration/friendship/hostility, prior advice/actions, material witness status and stake in outcome. Direct manager, subordinate or close collaborator of the subject is not an independent default; exclude material dependence and use an unconflicted internal or external route. Office of COO members receive NO automatic OIO authority; this target bars active Office appointees from QORP assignments to avoid operating/protected-role collisions.
4. Confirm consent, remaining case effort, actual accepted windows, released ordinary tasks and the independent investigation/adjudication/appeal route. Host manager does not choose case outcome. If no adequate availability, record blocker and seek another screened candidate.
5. Proper appointor issues case-specific mandate: category, case/question, permitted powers, minimum file, confidentiality, initial judge/appeal route where relevant, access expiry and next update. Ordinary assignment uses unconflicted Commissioner; Commissioner/OIO/COO/Owner cases keep the existing external/Owner-panel routes. QORP cannot cure an implicated selector.
6. Case owner approves minimum named access; qualified custodian executes/checks actual restrictions. No access before valid assignment, no general OIO or admin access. Default case access at most 30 days, renewal only with reasons/conflict check; eligibility's six-month term does not extend case access. Unsafe storage/access means blocked sensitive work.
7. Reserve accepts handover/evidence index, records independent work and reasons through the canonical case procedure. Investigator does not bind sanction; appellate and assurance roles remain separate.
8. On withdrawal/recusal/expiry, preserve authorized records, notify parties appropriately, log remaining work, appoint qualified replacement through same checks and revoke access. No obligation to keep volunteering; do not abandon interim restriction reviews. Case owner records completion, execution/refusal, record corrections, retention and removal of access.

The [case lifecycle](case-handling.md) remains canonical for proof, notice, response, sanctions, appeals, evidence and retention. QORP owns reserve eligibility/activation only; no competing sanction system is created.

## Capacity and safe reduced service

Aim for a primary-plus-qualified-backup route for each critical function, not a universal roster minimum. A reserve can cover several qualifications only in DIFFERENT unconflicted cases and within one capacity budget. Do not add their hours separately to each capability. Check simultaneous coverage, not just names on lists.

Commissioner tracks remaining effort by intake, investigation, initial adjudication, appeal and assurance separately in existing case registers. Use staffed urgency targets in the case policy. Prioritize current safety/restrictive measures, seek screened external help, consolidate genuinely common appeal questions while preserving individual facts, defer optional assurance and reduce unrelated optional services to release WILLING qualified people. Appeal eligibility never closes because the queue is full. Share only non-identifying capacity status with Planning/COO; small counts can reveal people and should be withheld.

At 20, internal reserves are easily conflicted; external referral or blocked merits review may be the honest result. At 30, a small assessed internal reserve set can help ordinary unrelated cases but does not solve executive/OIO conflicts. At 60, distinct existing contributors can carry investigation, appeal and assurance reserve qualifications with reduced delivery; no new permanent manager or person is added. [Fixtures](../capacity-readiness-v1-5.md) show hypothetical capacity only. If primary and reserve fail together or all are conflicted, log **blocked**, notify safe next update, preserve minimally, review restrictions and use valid external/platform referral; never self-review to meet a deadline.

## Minimal reserve/case record

```text
Category / assessment evidence / unconflicted assessor:
Valid registration authorizer / status / consent / review-expiry:
Case question / appointor / subject and reporting/prior-role conflicts:
Accepted case windows and effort / released ordinary work:
Distinct investigator / initial judge / appeal / assurance where required:
Actual safe storage/access owner / scoped mandate / access expiry:
Next update / handover or withdrawal / revocation / remaining blocker:
```

Use existing capability, assignment and case records with linked IDs, not duplicate forms. The extra category/conflict/expiry fields prevent unqualified substitutions, self-review and stale access; no daily reserve activity report is required.
