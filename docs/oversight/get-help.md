# Get help, report misconduct or appeal

**Project version:** 1.3.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R13 target adaptation pending ratification.** Existing case separation, confidentiality, volunteer consent and technical approval controls remain; no cutover is evidenced.

**Staff-facing navigation, not a new case charter.** You can raise a concern without reading the full investigation manual or obtaining your manager's permission. Use a published local contact; this repository contains no real intake address or available reviewer roster. Never submit personal evidence through a GitHub issue, commit or public channel.

## Choose the right help

| You need… | Start with… |
|---|---|
| Help with today's task | Your assigned task lead |
| A break, workload change, training or ordinary coaching | Your permanent Home People Manager; P&C for an alternative unconflicted support contact |
| Help with an ordinary player violation | Qualified Trust & Safety enforcement |
| Independent review of a decision affecting you | OIO Appeals & Member Advocacy |
| To report staff abuse, retaliation, corruption or fabricated evidence | OIO Internal Affairs |
| To raise a repeated systemic control/process defect | OIO Standards & Assurance |
| To suggest an improvement without a confidential complaint | Community Experience / Player Voice |

These routes summarize the [canonical referral matrix](independent-oversight.md#one-intake-one-case-owner--jurisdiction-and-referrals). A single event can involve a ban appeal and a distinct staff-abuse question; OIO links the questions without everyone retrying the same case.

## Report staff misconduct

Send the confidential OIO intake contact or protected IA lead:

- A safe way to reply and the relevant account IDs.
- What happened, approximately when, and the decision/rule involved if known.
- Relevant message/case links and any immediate safety or retaliation concern.

You do not have to prove guilt before reporting. Anonymous reports can be considered, although missing clarification may limit findings. A good-faith unproven complaint is not automatically malicious. Do not forward the complaint through the accused manager or post allegations publicly.

For a Commissioner complaint, bypass Commissioner screening to Owner/designated unconflicted intake and the external-review route. For an Owner complaint, contact a protected IA/Appeals lead; external findings cannot compel the Discord Owner. If the receiving officer is involved or closely connected, state the conflict and ask for an unconflicted alternate. If none exists, independent review may be blocked; nobody should claim otherwise.

## Request an appeal

Send OIO Appeals the decision/reference and receipt date, what material error or unfairness you dispute, relevant evidence and the correction requested. Grounds may include factual error, unfair procedure, conflict, disproportionate outcome or significant new evidence. Do not seek approval from the person whose decision you challenge.

The released v1.1 specification proposes a normal **14-calendar-day** submission window, reasonable late exceptions and independent remedies. **GOV-02 is still pending**: ask OIO which actual approved terms/mandate apply, and submit promptly rather than waiting for ratification. The reviewer must be uninvolved; their authority comes from a valid appeal mandate, not a higher rank. A promotion appeal can seek fair reassessment, not demand a nonexistent vacancy.

## What happens next

Intake acknowledges and routes the question, screens conflicts and identifies the proper case owner. IA investigates facts where misconduct is alleged; a separate adjudicator decides consequences; an uninvolved Appeals reviewer handles appeal. Assurance examines systems separately. Parties should receive appropriate reasons and a chance to respond; serious measures cannot be decided through a popularity vote or routine coaching note.

The full [case-handling specification](case-handling.md) owns notice periods, proof, protective measures, remedies, capacity targets and records. Its new powers require GOV-02 adoption. Outcomes may include correction, no established finding, coaching, proportionate sanction under valid authority, referral or a documented capacity/adoption blocker. Service targets are not guarantees and volunteers are not continuously on call.

## Privacy, conflicts and procedural concerns

Ask who will receive the material and use the approved restricted route before sharing sensitive attachments. Case access is need-to-know; rank, executive office, technical administration or Council membership is not blanket organizational authorization. Discord Owner/Administrator technical visibility remains a real limitation. Do not promise absolute secrecy or put completed records in this repository.

Raise investigator/reviewer involvement or retaliation with an uninvolved protected lead and request the alternate route. The full specification includes independent intake/closure challenges and appeal of material procedural errors; no officer should decide their own conflict objection. If the route lacks adoption, qualified people or safe storage, ask that the blocker and next safe contact/update be stated explicitly.

## Immediate danger or security compromise

Alert available qualified safety/technical responders and pause unsafe activity within your own authority. Report relevant content directly using Discord/Roblox tools; credible imminent danger may warrant local emergency services. Do not wait for internal case approval, collect unnecessary personal details or copy suspected illegal sexual material. See [serious-safety reporting](../operations/incident-response.md#serious-safety-and-platform-escalation).
