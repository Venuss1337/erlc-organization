# Case handling, adjudication and appeals

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Existing case separation, confidentiality, volunteer consent and technical approval controls remain; no cutover is evidenced.

Only reporting a concern or requesting an appeal? Use the short [reporting and appeal guide](get-help.md). Authorized case officers still use this full procedure and its adoption gates.

**Released procedure specification — adoption not verified.** This is the canonical case procedure supporting the [OIO charter](independent-oversight.md). New sanction and remedy powers activate only after **GOV-02** in the [ratification package](../proposals/v1-1-ratification.md) is approved and named, qualified case officers and restricted storage are available. Until then use existing valid authority, preserve evidence, provide safety referrals, and record any blocked adjudication or appeal. ## First response and procedure size

Anyone may submit a complaint privately to OIO intake or a protected functional lead. A complaint about the Commissioner bypasses the Commissioner to the Owner and the external-review route below. An Owner complaint bypasses the Owner's intake screening to the IA or Appeals lead. Support transfers only the contact and minimum description, never a public complaint thread. Anonymous reports may be considered; inability to seek clarification may limit findings. Good-faith reports, witness cooperation, appeals and criticism are protected from retaliation; a rejected or unproven complaint is not evidence of malice.

- **Short route:** A correctable procedural error or uncontested low-impact enforcement appeal needs one record: issue and evidence, conflict check, the affected person's response, named decision owner, reason, correction and independent challenge route. A manager may coach without a misconduct finding. A reviewer can correct a clear mistaken ban within delegated appeal authority. Do not open IA merely for an honest mistake.
- **Full route:** Use the lifecycle below for disputed staff misconduct, contested serious sanctions, retaliation, fabrication, senior-office complaints or significant safety/privacy harm. A denial of wrongdoing alone is not a reason to skip investigation. No short-route dismissal from staff or punitive rank reduction.
- **Safety first:** Immediate containment uses [incident response](../operations/incident-response.md); it does not establish guilt. Platform reports never wait for an internal case.

## Lifecycle and decision ownership

| Stage | Responsible role | Required action and output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Submit and acknowledge | OIO intake contact | Give case ID and confidential contact route; collect only account ID, allegation, relevant dates/links and requested help. Offer an accessible private text route. |
| 2. Classify jurisdiction | Unconflicted Commissioner or intake alternate | Route player enforcement to T&S, contested outcomes to Appeals, alleged staff misconduct to IA, systemic weaknesses to Assurance, ordinary coaching to the home manager. Link different questions; do not duplicate inquiries. |
| 3. Screen conflicts | Each proposed case officer; assigning officer verifies | Record involvement, reporting dependence, friendships/hostility, family/personal relationships and stake in outcome; decide recusal before access. The subject cannot approve assignments. |
| 4. Accept, refer or reject | IA lead for misconduct; Appeals lead for appeal; Assurance lead for audit | State jurisdiction, reviewable question and reasons. Lack of evidence permits proportionate inquiry, not a presumption of guilt. Give a rejection reason and independent intake-review route. |
| 5. Assign | Unconflicted Commissioner, or special appointor below | Name investigator, separate adjudicator and independent appeal route before full investigation; record scope, powers, access and expiry. If a later appeal reviewer is not yet named, reserve a demonstrably feasible alternate route. Otherwise label capacity blocked. |
| 6. Preserve and gather | Investigator; authorized evidence custodian | Create evidence index with source IDs, UTC capture time, collector, original context and access/transfer log. Preserve relevant originals in authorized storage; restrict copies, note missing/deleted material and challenges to authenticity. Follow the [retention schedule](../operations/discord-permissions.md#records-retention-and-deletion). |
| 7. Notify and respond | Investigator for allegations; adjudicator for proposed serious outcome | Give the subject allegations, applicable rule at the time, material evidence or fair redacted summary, possible outcomes and response route. Normally allow seven calendar days; extend for genuine availability/access needs. Record any safety-driven notification delay and review it at least every seven days. |
| 8. Findings | Investigator | Test both incriminating and exculpatory material, seek clarification, distinguish agreed facts, disputed facts and inference. Report findings and limitations; no binding sanction. |
| 9. Adjudicate | Separate authority in matrix below | Check process, evidence and response; request additional inquiry through investigator if needed. Give a further response opportunity for new material allegations. Decide each allegation, standard met/not met, reasons and proportionate outcome. |
| 10. Communicate and execute | Adjudicator; named line/technical executor | Give subject full reasons with necessary redactions, complainant an appropriate outcome summary, and appeal instructions. Executor changes access/status only within actual technical authority and logs completion or obstruction. No public shaming. |
| 11. Appeal | Uninvolved Appeals reviewer or appointed external reviewer | Recheck conflict screen and scope; consider submissions and original record. Issue independent reasons and remedy under the appeal section. Investigator and initial adjudicator cannot approve or veto the result. |
| 12. Correct and close | Personnel/technical executor; case owner | Correct linked records and notify affected recipients; confirm execution, remaining actions, retention/deletion dates, access revocation and closure notice. Assurance may later assess systems through a separate unconflicted assessor. |

Intake rejection or closure without adjudication may be challenged to an uninvolved Appeals reviewer within 14 days of notice. They can order intake reconsideration, referral or reasons; cannot conjure missing evidence or technical powers.

R14-J proposes a qualified initial-adjudicator pool below. Until its actual activation, existing valid adjudication mandates control; do not infer powers from an Office title. Investigation, adjudication, appeal and assurance stay separate.

## Disciplinary authority matrix

This matrix assigns **initial adjudication**, not investigation or final appeal. The Commissioner appoints the investigator and appeal reviewer subject to the special routes below, but does not select the factual outcome. Determine the row by the office implicated, not merely grade; a Senior Administrator without an executive appointment follows the ordinary staff/manager row. No disciplinary decision by the Council as a group.

| Subject / office implicated | Initial adjudicator and source of appointment | Conflict fallback and execution limits |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary division staff; Team Lead; working People Lead (Office appointments/Technical Office use senior-pool row) | Home Division Head, or an unconflicted Head specifically delegated by that Division Head in the case record | Commissioner assigns an unconflicted Head from another division if original Head is implicated, a witness or dependent on the subject. Delegation cannot include the investigator. |
| Division Heads; Technical Lead; Office appointments, including CoS and Integration Director; Technical Office staff | Unconflicted senior pool adjudicator selected case-by-case by Commissioner under R14-J | No automatic COO/Integration adjudication. Actual appointor executes reserved appointment changes; external appeal for senior office cases; no self-review. |
| Elected Staff Representative, where seat removal is sought | Independent external adjudicator under GOV-01/GOV-02 | Ordinary work coaching does not remove the elected mandate. A contested seat removal requires the separate charter safeguards; no unilateral home-manager or COO dismissal. |
| COO | Owner | External investigator and external appeal reviewer are mandatory; Owner cannot hear appeal of own decision. Only actual Owner executes removal of COO. |
| OIO personnel / protected functional lead | Unconflicted external adjudicator appointed by Owner | Unconflicted Commissioner administers assignment, not a subordinate of the subject; investigator and appeal reviewer external to implicated function. |
| Oversight Commissioner | Owner, after external investigation | Owner appoints separate external investigator and appeal reviewer; no Commissioner intake veto. Removal safeguards in GOV-02 apply. |
| Owner/CEO, or Owner jointly implicated | Independent advisory panel under GOV-02 | No compulsory sanction against Owner. Other subjects receive separately assigned unconflicted adjudication where possible. Publish only a privacy-safe process/outcome summary; explicitly label any refused remedy or blocked review. |

If any listed authority is conflicted or unavailable, use the alternate procedure; do not fall back to the accused's subordinate. A conflict affecting the Owner as selector uses the unconflicted GOV-02 panel selectors, not Owner self-clearance, even if Owner is not the principal accused. Owner-reserved appointment changes remain with the actual Owner even where external review reaches binding findings for ordinary delegated matters. A refusal to execute is logged as unresolved, never as completion. Until GOV-02, external findings/remedies remain advisory unless valid existing delegation specifically authorizes them.

## Qualified initial adjudicator pool

**Proposed R14-J + GOV-02; no actual pool is evidenced.** Actual Owner approves senior-adjudicator eligibility after Commissioner and an unconflicted protected lead assess evidence evaluation, fair notice/response, proportionate outcomes, confidentiality, reasoned writing, relevant domain literacy and a scenario exercise. Grade 05 or equivalent demonstrated senior judgment is eligibility, not automatic power. Qualified unconflicted Heads, Office specialists or screened outsiders may qualify; an operational appointment alone is insufficient. Ordinary staff normally use the unconflicted Head row; serious senior/Office matters use the pool.

Eligibility lasts at most six months, rechecked on role change. The Commissioner selects an available unconflicted officer for each case and records an explicit case delegation, scope, expiry and independent appeal route. The subject, investigator, prior decision adviser or dependent subordinate cannot select/control their own adjudicator. Screen reporting dependence, friendship, prior operational arbitration and personal stake; shared organizational membership alone neither proves nor cures conflict. Parties may raise objections through the existing alternate procedure. A Commissioner conflict uses the existing special external appointor, not COO case control.

Pool membership grants no standing disciplinary command, personnel supervision or access to unrelated files. Case access lasts at most 30 days unless reasonedly renewed under the case policy. Removal from eligibility requires recorded capability/absence/confidentiality reasons and unconflicted advice; disagreement with findings is not grounds. Case replacement preserves records and party notice. Appointors execute reserved appointment remedies; if they refuse, log unresolved execution rather than call it completed. Pool delegation never transfers Owner powers.

COO cases still require Owner initial decision with separate external investigation/appeal. Commissioner/OIO and Owner cases retain the protected special routes below; this pool cannot bypass them. No willing qualified unconflicted adjudicator or feasible independent appeal means merits adjudication is blocked: preserve/referral, review interim restrictions and reduce optional services; do not assign the investigator or original actor. External availability remains an implementation dependency, not a fictional reserve.

## Evidence, fairness and outcomes

The organization bears the burden of establishing misconduct on the **balance of probabilities**: more likely than not on the available reliable evidence. This is an internal policy standard, not a criminal judgment. Serious allegations require careful scrutiny of reliability, context and plausible alternatives; severity does not lower the standard. Rank, popularity, silence, anonymous accusations or a raw screenshot alone are not automatic proof. Where authenticity or material facts remain unresolved, record **not established / insufficient evidence**, rather than declaring innocence proved or guilt assumed. Restrictive protective measures need a separate recorded risk justification.

Disclose enough substance for a meaningful response, with witness/privacy redactions and a support person if desired and unconflicted. Do not promise anonymity that cannot be maintained. If nondisclosure makes a decisive allegation impossible to answer fairly, seek safer corroboration, limit reliance or close that allegation; do not use secret decisive evidence as an easy shortcut. Volunteers may decline interviews; decide only on available evidence without inventing an employment duty or punishment for exercising an appeal. Investigation confidentiality is need-to-know, not a ban on reporting danger or seeking appropriate support.

| Conduct / problem | Normal response | Escalation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Training gap, good-faith mistake, ordinary poor management | Coaching, corrected record, supervised practice or non-punitive reassignment under [management review](../personnel/management-and-development.md) | No automatic misconduct finding or grade penalty |
| Established minor misconduct or avoidable negligent rule breach | Documented warning, relevant retraining, scoped conditions; explain why coaching alone insufficient | Repetition matters only if comparable, communicated and still relevant |
| Established repeated misconduct, serious negligence or material authority misuse | Final warning, temporary restriction, removal from a specific assignment, or proportionate retained-grade reduction where its basis is invalidated | State start/end or review date, scope, re-entry criteria and appeal |
| Established serious abuse, retaliation, corruption, deliberate evidence fabrication or severe safety harm | Immediate removal from appointment/staff and necessary community restriction may be proportionate | No requirement to exhaust warnings first; platform referral where appropriate, separate internal fact-finding |

Choose the least restrictive effective response considering harm, intent, recurrence, responsibility, mitigation and consistency with comparable cases. Do not invent fines, compulsory hours, humiliation or ban quotas. A record of warning remains actionable for six months from final decision, unless a reasoned decision sets a shorter period; older retained evidence may inform a new inquiry but is not an automatic penalty multiplier. Appointment-linked grade reversion is administrative, distinct from a punitive reduction of retained standing.

An unconflicted line authority in the matrix may temporarily remove implicated duty/access where credible risk of ongoing harm, retaliation or evidence interference exists. Notify OIO, give reasons and an independent challenge route; review within 72 hours and at least every seven days. Urgent qualified responders may contain first and refer immediately. These are protective measures, not a finding; expiry or continuation must be recorded. Pending appeal does not automatically restore unsafe access, but the appeal reviewer can stay or narrow measures. Absence of review staff is not a justification for indefinite suspension: escalate capacity and consider a less restrictive arrangement.

## Independent appeal and remedies

An affected subject may appeal an enforcement, grade, appointment, adverse performance or disciplinary decision within **14 calendar days of receipt**. A complainant may challenge rejection, material procedural failure, conflict, retaliation or significant new evidence; they have no right to demand a particular punishment. Accept a late submission where absence, safety, inaccessible notice or other reasonable obstacle explains delay. No fee, compulsory public statement or minimum rank.

Grounds: material factual error, procedural unfairness, undisclosed conflict, disproportionate outcome, inconsistent application or significant new evidence unavailable earlier. Intake checks grounds and jurisdiction; do not reject merely because the original authority disagrees. Duplicate appeals about the same decision may share an evidence review; preserve each person's materially different facts, confidentiality and response. One reasoned appeal determination is normally final internally; significant new evidence or procedural corruption may justify reopening by a different reviewer, with reasons.

After GOV-02 takes effect, a properly delegated unconflicted appeal reviewer may uphold, vary, cancel or stay a delegated outcome; correct records; restore eligible assignment/access subject to current qualifications and technical approval; order a fresh assessment or rehearing by different officers; and refer separate misconduct to IA. No penalty increase on appeal without new alleged misconduct, notice and a separate fair adjudication. Reserved appointments/ownership remedies remain recommendations to Owner, who must record acceptance/refusal. A successful promotion appeal normally orders fair reassessment, not an automatic appointment to a nonexistent vacancy.

Reviewer issues a concise decision explaining scope, material facts, reasoning, remedy, executor, execution target and further platform reporting options. A correction must propagate to personnel/enforcement records, with a visible link from the superseded record and restricted audit history; never silently erase wrongdoing or retain an incorrect public accusation.

## Alternates and external reviewers

No new permanent units are created. Commissioner maintains a restricted capability/availability list, reviewed on change and monthly when cases are open. Seek at least one reserve for investigation, adjudication, appeal and assurance; the same reserve may qualify for several functions but cannot combine incompatible roles in one case. An empty list is an explicit readiness blocker.

1. **Eligibility:** Demonstrated relevant assessment using a fictional case, confidentiality/evidence training, sound judgment, consent and actual availability. No moderation prerequisite. Screen current and recent reporting dependence, personal links, previous advice and financial/community stakes; outsiders are not automatically independent. No sensitive access until screened.
2. **Ordinary assignment:** Unconflicted Commissioner selects a qualified alternate with the relevant functional lead's capability advice; written mandate names case, role, independence, powers, permitted records and expiry. If Commissioner implicated, Owner appoints external officers. For other OIO subjects, Owner authorizes external assignment. An adjudicator's powers require the matrix delegation, not merely reviewer-list membership.
3. **External selection:** Commissioner proposes at least two suitable consenting candidates where feasible; Owner appoints for reserved/senior/OIO conflict cases after independent functional advice. Record reasons if only one candidate. Disclose identity and conflict summary to parties; allow reasoned objections before substantive review, normally within three days. An uninvolved Appeals lead assesses ordinary assignment objections; OIO-lead/Commissioner objections go to a separate external assessor appointed by Owner; Owner-case objections go to the panel's uninvolved selector under GOV-02. The challenged officer never resolves their own objection.
4. **Mandate and access:** State binding delegated or advisory status, question, limits, confidentiality undertaking, safe contact, minimum redacted file, no onward sharing and return/deletion. Grant named case access only, not server administration; limit to 30 days, renew in writing only for continuing need with conflict recheck. Revoke on recusal, completion, loss of qualification or expiry. Emergency continuation permits safety referral and evidence preservation, not indefinite case access.
5. **Replacement:** Appointor can replace an unavailable, conflicted or confidentiality-breaching reviewer with recorded reasons, independent functional advice and notice to parties. Disagreement with an emerging finding is not grounds for removal; preserve original record and report interference to an uninvolved functional lead/external assessor. Replacement does not rewrite findings secretly.
6. **No reviewer available:** Publish only an anonymized capacity status, notify parties privately, seek consent to defer with a next update, protect evidence, reduce optional services to release willing qualified staff, and seek another screened outsider. No conflicted substitute or invented review. Review interim restrictions and expedite platform safety reporting. A case may remain blocked; document why.

Owner-related panels use the specific selection and limitations in GOV-02; the ordinary Owner appointment route cannot be used to make the subject select their own independent panel.

## Triage, capacity and service targets

Targets are calendar periods when staffed, not guaranteed completion times or compulsory volunteer shifts. Intake publishes a safe fallback/reporting route if unattended; it never promises 24/7 response.

| Priority | Examples | Target / action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 immediate danger/security | Ongoing abuse, credible imminent danger, evidence destruction, privileged compromise | Available qualified responders contain/report immediately; notify OIO and leadership as soon as safely possible. Do not wait for intake acknowledgement. |
| P1 urgent fairness/safety | Active restrictive measure, retaliation risk, senior interference | Acknowledge within 24 hours when staffed; screen/assign or report capacity blocker within 72 hours. |
| P2 ordinary case | Contested historical action, nonurgent misconduct or grade appeal | Acknowledge within three days; accept/refer/reject or explain need for clarification within seven. |
| P3 systemic / low-impact correction | Historical process improvement, routine assurance | Acknowledge within seven days; schedule through exception register. |

Aim for full initial findings/decision within 21 days and appeal determination within 21 days after acceptance; update parties every seven days on open P1/P2 cases, explaining delays and next check. Safety and complexity override simplistic closure targets. Ask for only estimated remaining effort and volunteered review capacity, not intimate availability details.

Commissioner checks open cases weekly: age, urgency, independent officers, next action, estimated remaining effort and any protective restriction review. Trigger alternate recruitment/reallocation when no unconflicted officer exists, a target is missed without a plan, or accepted work exceeds the capacity officers consent to provide before the next review. Record a reduced service notice and protect time-sensitive appeals before optional assurance work; never drop safety reporting or suspend appeal eligibility because the queue is full. Commissioner may coordinate workload but cannot dictate outcomes or obtain blanket evidence access.
