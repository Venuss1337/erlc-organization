# Roleplay Programs Directorate

**Project version:** 1.2.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

Canonical home for this directorate's mandate and internal structure. [Executive reporting](../governance/structure.md), [grade and eligibility rules](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md), [staff assignments](../personnel/assignments.md) and [independent referrals](../oversight/independent-oversight.md) remain in their respective homes. Staffing bands are illustrative, not minimum headcounts.

**Director:** Director of Roleplay Programs ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight))  
**Reports to:** Executive Director — Operations ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight); portfolio appointment)  
**Mandate:** Department programs, player-facing RP opportunities, coordinated standards.

| Program | Structure | Staffing pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Law Enforcement Program** | Program Commander → Deputies / Unit Supervisors → RP personnel | Expandable large roster |
| **Fire & EMS Program** | Program Commander → Deputies / Unit Supervisors → RP personnel | Expandable large roster |
| **Transportation / DOT Program** | DOT Program Coordinator → Supervisors / RP personnel | Coordinator-led until growth |
| **Civilian RP Program** | Civilian Program Lead → RP Coordinators / participants | Flexible |
| **Roleplay Standards** | RP Standards Lead or Coordinator → Evaluators / Trainers | Compact: approx. 1–3 |

**Crucial distinction:** Roleplay department members are generally **players**, not automatically organizational staff. A police department's roleplay rank is separate from a Discord staff qualification, team membership, or personnel authority.

**Owns:** Department standards, membership systems, departmental qualifications, internal RP promotions and RP programming.  
**Does not own:** General Discord moderation, global bans, server infrastructure, or independent staff-misconduct investigations. **Roleplay Standards** sets RP-specific vehicle, uniform, conduct and qualification rules; independent Standards & Assurance examines systemic compliance and governance failures rather than running RP standards day-to-day.

## Role profiles

These profiles apply to actual organizational staff appointments, not every RP player. [Rank eligibility](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight) and the actual home/assignment record control; RP rank never supplies general moderation powers.

| Position / rank relationship | Home and permanent supervisor | Main work and independent decisions | Qualification / limits / escalation and review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Program Commander / Deputy / Unit Supervisor, 01 baseline; 04 for organizational management | Law Enforcement, Fire & EMS or recorded RP program; Director or recorded home program manager | Program delivery, internal RP membership/promotion within approved criteria | Program qualification and explicit role remit; no global bans or Discord administration. Program exceptions to RP Director; staff abuse/independent appeal to OIO |
| DOT Coordinator / Civilian Lead, 01 baseline unless substantive management | Relevant program; RP Director/recorded home manager | Coordinate agreed program activity within standards | Program competence; solo responsibility need not create a manager. Uncovered activity to Director; safety issue to qualified responder |
| RP Standards Lead / Evaluator / Trainer, 01 baseline; 04 if real unit manager | Roleplay Standards; Director/recorded manager | Assess RP vehicles, uniforms, qualifications and training standards | RP assessment competence; does not audit entire organization or investigate staff abuse. Repeated systemic control fault to Assurance |
