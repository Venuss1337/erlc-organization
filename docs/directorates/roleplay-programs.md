# Roleplay Programs — Game & Roleplay Operations

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

Accountable owner: **Game & Roleplay Operations Division Head**, directly reporting to COO. Preserved functions: **Law Enforcement; Fire & EMS; DOT; Civilian RP; Roleplay Standards**. Each function has one named working coordinator; this is not a separate supervisory office. Actual People Manager is the Head or recorded working People Lead (Technical Lead for office staff). All original function scope transfers under the [complete map](../operations/function-map.md).

The accountable owner owns functional standards, capacity and appointment decisions within [delegated authority](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md). Coordinators route work, not personnel discipline. Role-specific qualifications remain required; rank grants no blanket powers. Cross-function coordination stays with the functional owner/Head. Heads cooperate bilaterally; only unresolved covered cross-division questions use the [Office mandate](../governance/office-of-the-coo.md#binding-integration-delegation), not an added reporting layer. Protected complaints and appeals go to [OIO](../oversight/independent-oversight.md).

RP participants remain players unless appointed to actual organizational work. Program Commanders own program decisions and training; session command does not override RP standards or qualified enforcement.

Historical filename retained for stable repository navigation; it does not retain the former Directorate Director appointment.

## Role profiles

| Position / grade | Home / permanent supervisor | Independent decisions and limits | Qualification / escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP Program Commander / trainer; 01; 04 only for substantive people/function management | Game & Roleplay Operations; recorded home People Lead or Game Head | Own assigned program standards/training, no generic Discord command; ordinary players not staff | Program-specific assessment, safe instruction and records; task issue to assigned lead, scope/capacity to accountable owner, misconduct/appeal to OIO |
| Working People Lead where justified | Game & Roleplay Operations; accountable owner | Coaching, accepted work/coverage and handoff; no second home boss for secondary staff | Relevant management/domain qualification; [decision matrix](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) and [continuity](../operations/continuity.md) control consequential decisions |

Heads/Technical Lead report directly to COO. Integration can decide only its adopted domain; it does not supervise them. One agreed record replaces duplicate Office requests.

## RP Programs functional ownership

A qualified working RP Programs owner coordinates published program administration, shared training calendar and routine RP standards; named LE/Fire-EMS/DOT/Civilian owners execute within assessed expertise. They report task outcomes to Game Head but gain no permanent personnel reports. [Canonical matrix](../operations/management-capacity.md#binding-functional-responsibility-matrix) owns scope/backup rules. New policy and personnel sanctions remain outside this function; qualification gaps pause the affected program rather than create a new Director layer.
