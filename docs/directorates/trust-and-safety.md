# Trust & Safety Division

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

Accountable owner: **Trust & Safety Division Head**, directly reporting to COO. Preserved functions: **Discord Moderation; Game Enforcement; Case Operations; Critical Incident Response**. Each function has one named working coordinator; this is not a separate supervisory office. Actual People Manager is the Head or recorded working People Lead (Technical Lead for office staff). All original function scope transfers under the [complete map](../operations/function-map.md).

The accountable owner owns functional standards, capacity and appointment decisions within [delegated authority](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md). Coordinators route work, not personnel discipline. Role-specific qualifications remain required; rank grants no blanket powers. Cross-function coordination stays with the functional owner/Head. Heads cooperate bilaterally; only unresolved covered cross-division questions use the [Office mandate](../governance/office-of-the-coo.md#binding-integration-delegation), not an added reporting layer. Protected complaints and appeals go to [OIO](../oversight/independent-oversight.md).


Historical filename retained for stable repository navigation; it does not retain the former Directorate Director appointment.

## Role profiles

| Position / grade | Home / permanent supervisor | Independent decisions and limits | Qualification / escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enforcement Officer / senior officer; 02 / 03 current enforcement | Trust & Safety; recorded home People Lead or T&S Head | Published action scope, evidence and lasting-ban delegation; no independent IA/Appeals | Current enforcement and duty-IC assessment are separate; task issue to assigned lead, scope/capacity to accountable owner, misconduct/appeal to OIO |
| Working People Lead where justified | Trust & Safety; accountable owner | Coaching, accepted work/coverage and handoff; no second home boss for secondary staff | Relevant management/domain qualification; [decision matrix](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) and [continuity](../operations/continuity.md) control consequential decisions |

Heads/Technical Lead report directly to COO. Integration can decide only its adopted domain; it does not supervise them. One agreed record replaces duplicate Office requests.

## Working functional owners

Qualified contributors own enforcement rotation/quality, readiness and training within published scope under the [capacity matrix](../operations/management-capacity.md#binding-functional-responsibility-matrix). People Leads protect coaching time before accepting frontline shifts. T&S quality checks are NOT independent Appeals or staff-misconduct IA. If functional workload cannot be accepted safely, reduce optional patrol/session commitments; no new management tier or punishment quota.
