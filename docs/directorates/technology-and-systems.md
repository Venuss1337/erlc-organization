# Technology & Systems Directorate

**Project version:** 1.2.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

Canonical home for this directorate's mandate and internal structure. [Executive reporting](../governance/structure.md), [grade and eligibility rules](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md), [staff assignments](../personnel/assignments.md) and [independent referrals](../oversight/independent-oversight.md) remain in their respective homes. Staffing bands are illustrative, not minimum headcounts.

**Director:** Director of Technology & Systems ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight))  
**Reports to:** Executive Director — Community & Services ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight); portfolio appointment)  
**Mandate:** Dependable Discord platform, bot automation, data reporting and access implementation.

| Team or function | Roles | Staffing pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform Engineering** | Technical Lead → Bot Developer / Systems Developer | Approx. 1–4 |
| **Infrastructure & Access** | Systems Administrator / Access Coordinator | Approx. 1–2 |
| **Operational Analytics** | Data & Reporting Analyst | One person initially; approx. 1–2 |

These may share one lead when compact. Technical security implementation belongs here; **independent audit of security controls** belongs to the Office of Independent Oversight’s Standards & Assurance function.

**Owns:** Bots, integrations, roles/channels configuration, permission execution, server reliability, data/reporting tools.  
**Does not own:** Changing organizational policy by editing Discord permissions; unreviewed acquisition of broad case evidence.

## Role profiles

Summaries only; [eligibility](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight), [technical approvals](../operations/discord-permissions.md#access-approval-execution-and-review) and actual assignment/access govern. Confirm one home People Manager; a technical title is not Discord Administrator permission.

| Position / rank relationship | Home and permanent supervisor | Main work and independent decisions | Qualification / limits / escalation and review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developer / Systems Developer, 01 baseline or assessed management-equivalent 04 | Platform Engineering; recorded Technical Lead/Director | Implement authorized work; make delegated technical implementation choices | Technical competence and scoped access; no self-approved privileges, policy edits through permissions or unrelated case access. Approval gaps to Director; emergency to authorized custodian |
| Technical Lead / Systems Administrator job, 04 for real function management; otherwise specialist 01 | Recorded technical function; Technology Director | Coordinate technical function and approved changes within mandate | Relevant technical/management competence and separate access authorization. Independent privilege approval/execution and restoration checks remain; Assurance audits systems |
| Access Coordinator / Data Analyst, 01 baseline unless assessed equivalent | Infrastructure & Access or Operational Analytics; recorded Lead/Director | Execute approved access or produce appropriate aggregate reporting | Access/privacy/data competence; never read private cases just because technically possible. Case visibility to unconflicted case owner; systemic concerns to Assurance, misconduct to IA |
