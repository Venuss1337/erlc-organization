# Technology & Systems Directorate

**Project version:** 1.1.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

Canonical home for this directorate's mandate and internal structure. [Executive reporting](../governance/structure.md), [grade and eligibility rules](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md), [staff assignments](../personnel/assignments.md) and [independent referrals](../oversight/independent-oversight.md) remain in their respective homes. Staffing bands are illustrative, not minimum headcounts.

**Director:** Director of Technology & Systems ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight))  
**Reports to:** Executive Director — Community & Services ([grade 06 — Director](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight); portfolio appointment)  
**Mandate:** Dependable Discord platform, bot automation, data reporting and access implementation.

| Team or function | Roles | Staffing pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform Engineering** | Technical Lead → Bot Developer / Systems Developer | Approx. 1–4 |
| **Infrastructure & Access** | Systems Administrator / Access Coordinator | Approx. 1–2 |
| **Operational Analytics** | Data & Reporting Analyst | One person initially; approx. 1–2 |

These may share one lead when compact. Technical security implementation belongs here; **independent audit of security controls** belongs to the Office of Independent Oversight’s Standards & Assurance function.

**Owns:** Bots, integrations, roles/channels configuration, permission execution, server reliability, data/reporting tools.  
**Does not own:** Changing organizational policy by editing Discord permissions; unreviewed acquisition of broad case evidence.

## v1.1 procedure dependencies

The [technical access, recovery and retention procedures](../operations/discord-permissions.md#access-approval-execution-and-review) own execution controls; [incident response](../operations/incident-response.md) owns coordination. Maintain actual implementation evidence separately; this documentation does not establish tested security. New procedure activation remains subject to the [approval register](../proposals/review-register.md).
