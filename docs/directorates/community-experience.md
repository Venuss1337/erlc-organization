Each function has one named working coordinator; this is not a separate supervisory office. Actual People Manager is the Head or recorded working People Lead (Technical Lead for office staff). All original function scope transfers under the [complete map](../operations/function-map.md).

The accountable owner owns functional standards, capacity and appointment decisions within [delegated authority](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md). Protected complaints and appeals go to [OIO](../oversight/independent-oversight.md).

Personnel/applicant/wellbeing files remain in separate need-to-know stores. Support, events and public feedback staff gain no personnel or case access through shared division membership. P&C process verification remains separate from substantive hiring selection.

Historical filename retained for stable repository navigation; it does not retain the former Directorate Director appointment.

## Player Voice & Public Improvement Framework

**Accountable owner:** Community & People Division Head. **Day-to-day Driver:** Player Feedback Coordinator, ordinarily an existing Community Engagement or Member Services contributor. **Decision owners:** The directorate responsible for each suggestion; the Governance Council only when a proposed change is organization-wide. This is a **process**, not an additional Council seat or mandatory managerial layer.

1. **Continuous intake:** Offer one accessible suggestion channel/form with categories for session experience, RP programs, Discord usability, events and general ideas. Allow private feedback when needed; send reports of abuse, bans and sensitive complaints to the proper case/appeal route instead of posting them publicly.
2. **Triage and ownership:** Merge duplicates, remove harassment/private data, distinguish bugs from ideas, assign one responsible directorate and record the next review point. Aim to acknowledge actionable submissions within **seven days when staffed**; this is a service target, not an enforceable promise.
3. **Monthly open consultation:** Run a short town-hall thread, voice Q&A, structured poll or equivalent on **one or two concrete topics**, with an asynchronous option for players who cannot attend. Publish the question, relevant constraints and a brief summary of arguments. Polls inform decisions; they do not automatically override safety rules or leadership authority.
4. **Quarterly pulse survey:** Ask a few repeatable questions about session quality, accessibility of activities, fairness, onboarding and priorities. Separate staff experience surveys from player surveys. Do not collect real names or unnecessary identifying data; use aggregated results and acknowledge selection bias.
5. **Public improvement tracker:** Publish a readable issue, description, responsible team, last update and status: `Received → Reviewing → Planned → In Progress → Implemented`, or `Declined / Not Now` with a concise reason. Not every suggestion becomes a promise. Sensitive abuse reports, evidence and personnel matters **never** appear on the public tracker.
6. **Close the loop:** Review open items at least **monthly**, publish a short `You said / We did / Why not` update, and link implemented changes to the original feedback. When multiple directorates or organization-wide policy are affected, submit a properly scoped proposal to the Governance Council.

**Fairness rule:** Players may criticize policies without retaliation. Moderation remains available for harassment, spam and private-information exposure. Do not publish individual disciplinary outcomes as proof that feedback was acted on.

**Success signals:** Number of actionable themes addressed, implementation/update timeliness, repeat player concerns, survey response coverage and player-reported experience; **not** raw suggestion counts or promises to fulfill every request. Public summaries must be understandable without internal management terminology.

## Role profiles

| Position / grade | Home / permanent supervisor | Independent decisions and limits | Qualification / escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support / feedback / partnership coordinator; 01 qualification-gated | Community & People; recorded home People Lead or Community Head | Own accepted requests and public feedback; protected complaints go to OIO, no personnel access by membership | Support/public communication, referral and privacy competence; task issue to assigned lead, scope/capacity to accountable owner, misconduct/appeal to OIO |
| Working People Lead where justified | Community & People; accountable owner | Coaching, accepted work/coverage and handoff; no second home boss for secondary staff | Relevant management/domain qualification; [decision matrix](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) and [continuity](../operations/continuity.md) control consequential decisions |

Heads/Technical Lead report directly to COO. Integration can decide only its adopted domain; it does not supervise them. One agreed record replaces duplicate Office requests.

## Capacity-aware community ownership

A qualified community service owner handles routine support, engagement and factual communication within scope. Separate restricted People ownership handles recruitment/records; shared Head does not merge access. [Capacity matrix](../operations/management-capacity.md#binding-functional-responsibility-matrix) controls qualifications, backup and decisions. Recruitment/support peaks require explicit reallocation or slower nonurgent support, not every ticket escalating to Head.
