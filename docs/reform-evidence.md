## Structural comparison

|---|---|---|
| Operating directorates / office | Six directorates | Three divisions plus compact Office |
| Permanent ED layer | Two offices | None |
| COO delivery reports | Two portfolios (or six Directors if inactive) | Four accountable delivery owners |
| People Managers | 18 in original audit fixture | Computed below from actual manager IDs |
| Maximum intermediate managers from contributor to COO | Lead → Manager → Director → ED: four | People Lead → Head: two |
| Same-function/home transfer approvals across former boundaries | Two Directors | One Head inside merged division; two across divisions |

The target reassigns people, not deletes contributors: work released from ED/Director/Unit Manager appointments goes into delivery, working coaching, technical resilience and case reserves. This is an illustrative allocation, not proof former incumbents consent or can perform new roles. Dedicated CoS headcount is not added. Technical mentoring remains a justified narrow span; Owner and Commissioner also have narrow spans for ownership/independence reasons.

## Small and growing models

20: Owner 1, COO 1, Safety 4, Game 5, Community 4, Technology 2, OIO 3. Heads are working contributors; no dedicated People Leads. 30: 1/1/7/9/6/3/3 respectively, with one working Game People Lead. 60: 1/1/16/21/15/3/3, with two working People Leads per large division. At 60 Game Leads supervise nine contributors each; this is a material volunteer coaching risk requiring accepted availability, subgroup mentoring without extra bosses, service reductions or a separately justified additional Lead. It is not hidden by the narrower chart.

The OIO trio cannot independently handle every conflict. Commissioner may undertake limited unconflicted systems work, never self-audit; use qualified independent reserves/external review or pause/refer. No reserve is assumed available. Two concurrent sessions require two separate qualified Commanders and enforcement responders in accepted overlapping windows; annual roster totals do not establish coverage.

## Paired adversarial tests

P = fresh approvals; H = modeled responsible-role handoffs, excluding notifications and routine recording. These are scenario paths, not universal minimums. Old optional EDs are active for the old full fixture; both designs require valid mandates and the same case safeguards.

| Input in both models | Old path P/H | Target path P/H | Outcome, burden and tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two simultaneous sessions, two Commanders/two responders available | Commanders launch 0/0 | Same 0/0 | No fabricated speed gain; if one responder absent, one session pauses in both |
| Session/RP resource dispute after leads fail | Two Directors → Ops ED 1/2 | Game Head 1/1 | One handoff removed; Head now handles a broader remit |
| Team leader unexpectedly unavailable | Director appoints scoped coverage 1/1 | Head appoints scoped coverage 1/1 | No layer saving; missing qualified relief blocks both |
| Five support requests, two confidential personnel matters | CE/P&C owners coordinate via two Directors 2/2 for shared allocation | Community Head decides allocation 1/1 | Fewer resource approvals; private records remain separately scoped; pooled staff cannot view People files |
| Recruit into approved vacancy during shortage | Hiring owner selects, Personnel verifies 1/2 | Same 1/2 | No extra executive permission; Head selection burden may rise |
| Division/Director alleged abuse | Unconflicted ED adjudicates after IA 2/3 including assignment | COO adjudicates after independent IA 2/3 | Fewer available senior adjudicators; external appeal/reserve essential; worse COO load |
| COO allegation | External investigation → Owner → independent external appeal 3/5 | Same 3/5 | Conditional on actual outsiders and Owner cooperation |
| Appeal reviewer conflicted, no reserve accepts | Pause/request external 1/1 administrative assignment attempt | Same 1/1 | Blocked merits review in both; never use same investigator |
| Privileged bot/account compromised above maintainer's role | Custodian containment → application owner → restoration approver 1/3 | Same 1/3 | Native hierarchy unchanged; absent custodian blocks recovery |
| Council 2 yes, 1 no, 2 abstentions | No adoption 0/0 | No adoption 0/0 | Target removes ED dependency, not affirmative thresholds; elected seats can still be vacant |
| Contested 04 promotion, connected assessor | Director decision → independent reassessment 2/2 | Head decision → independent reassessment 2/2 | No prestige shortcut; conflict screening unchanged |
| Three volunteers resign, including People Lead | Director/Manager reconstruct coverage 1/2 | Head reconstructs coverage 1/1 | One relay removed, broader Head workload; reduce sessions rather than compel survivors |
| Major session disruption requiring IC | Qualified containment/IC, later lasting review 1/2 | Same 1/2 | No executive preapproval; no IC final disciplinary powers |
| Developer challenged by unrelated manager | Technical Director → services ED → COO if unresolved 1/2 | Technical Lead → COO 1/1 | Clear direct specialist boundary; COO must understand risk or seek unconflicted expertise |
| Sustained high work across all functions | EDs absorb portfolio coordination, six Directors 1/2 per unresolved tradeoff | Four owners → COO 1/1 | Less relay but target worse if COO absent or Game/Community Head overloaded; pause optional work, no guaranteed success |

Transfer example: Host moves into RP training inside Game; former releasing Live Ops Director plus receiving RP Director approvals (2) become one Head approval with both home/host consultation (1). Consultation, qualification checks, consent, record and access controls remain. Across Safety and Game two approvals remain. No claim of reduced real-world processing time.

## Migration risks and audit readiness

Five residual risks: hostile Owner/blocked ratification; unavailable independent reserves; overloaded Game/Community Heads; technical custody/privacy implementation failures; unavailable COO/succession and actual accepted coverage. The narrower model trades specialist executive capacity for fewer boundaries. Do not deploy without actual appointments, adoption, privacy/access tests and staff consultation. Ready for an independent design audit, not certification of real operations.
