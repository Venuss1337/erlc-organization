## Organization chart and lines of accountability

```text
Owner / Chief Executive
├── Office of the Chief Operating Officer [functional grouping]
│   ├── COO [accountable operating executive]
│   ├── Director of Operations Integration [reports to COO]
│   ├── Chief of Staff [reports functionally to COO]
│   └── Operations Planning & Performance Lead [conditional; to COO]
│
│   Direct delivery reports to COO, NOT through Office colleagues:
│   ├── Head of Trust & Safety
│   ├── Head of Game & Roleplay Operations
│   ├── Head of Community & People
│   └── Technical Lead — Technology Office
└── Oversight Commissioner — Office of Independent Oversight
    ├── Internal Affairs & Investigations
    ├── Independent Appeals & Member Advocacy
    └── Standards & Assurance
Council: collective policy relationship, not another management superior
```

Office grouping is not a personnel reporting node or an extra approval stage. [Office charter](office-of-the-coo.md) is the sole full mandate for executive appointments and Integration decisions. Three divisions plus Technology Office remain; no permanent portfolio or Unit Manager layer returns. Owner's two principal accountability relationships remain COO and Commissioner.

## Management design and leadership profiles

A People Manager owns permanent coaching/development. A task lead directs accepted session, incident or project work. A coordinator owns a deliverable without automatically supervising staff. Exactly one permanent People Manager is recorded. A concurrent Office appointment has functional accountability to COO and a single home manager, with agreed allocation; a dedicated appointee's manager is COO.

| Role | Unique contribution / reporting | Independent decisions / limits |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Actual ownership; legitimate root | Reserved appointments/ratification, no invented transfer of ownership |
| COO | Operating integration and four delivery owners; to Owner | Reserved executive work; delegated decisions are not routinely reapproved |
| Office appointees | Specialized work; functional COO accountability | Only Office charter/recorded mandate; no universal Head command |
| Division Head | Functional ownership; directly COO | Staffing, standards, service capacity and internal priorities; protected cases separate |
| Technical Lead | Compact technical ownership; directly COO | Qualified technical choices and approved access scope; no self-approved elevation/restoration |
| Working People Lead | Coaching accepted contributors; to Head | Team tasks/coverage within scope; no second permanent boss for hosted staff |
| Operational Commander / coordinator | Specific accepted work; home manager retained | Session/incident/program mandate, not automatic permanent supervision |
| Commissioner | Protected OIO administration; directly Owner | No dictated findings or blanket case access |

## Activation and scale

20 staff: working Heads, qualified concurrent Office work and narrower services; no mandatory dedicated executive administration. 30: one Game People Lead if justified, Integration concurrent or substantive under actual workload. 60: two working Leads in each large division, dedicated Integration/CoS only when funded with real voluntary capacity; Planning conditional. [Staffing and workload evidence](../executive-readiness-v1-4.md) accounts for unique people and displaced delivery.

Heads continue bilateral decisions and preassigned domains. An unavailable Office member does not block routine division work. Working Leads' coaching load and broad Game/Community responsibilities remain capacity risks; reduce services, adjust allocations or seek a reasoned approved change. No manager appears solely because a headcount threshold is reached.
