## Five seats independent of optional offices

Owner, COO, elected operational leadership representative, elected functional representative from a different delivery area, elected frontline Staff Representative. Each person has one vote; OIO Commissioner and protected leads remain non-voting advisers. CoS is non-voting Secretary and may hold the appointment concurrently, but cannot also hold a voting seat.

Non-executive organizational staff elect all three representative seats by confidential one-person/one-vote ballots, seven-day nomination and seven-day ballot windows, plurality with runoff ties. Leadership representative candidates must hold actual working Head/People Lead/Technical Lead appointments; functional representative may be any qualified contributor with functional experience in a different area. Frontline candidate cannot be a permanent People Manager. No minimum grade or invented management appointment to fill seats. Active COO Office appointees (including concurrent holders) cannot occupy an elected seat; they resign that seat with elected-alternate coverage before Office activation. This preserves nonexecutive representation. Office titles add no votes. If no eligible leadership candidate, that seat remains vacant; no fabricated vote.

Six-month terms; publish eligibility, conflicts, turnout and totals without ballots. Elect ranked consenting alternates per seat; no person fills two seats. Vacancies trigger replacement election for remaining term; unconflicted elected alternate covers meanwhile. Representatives retain their home People Manager and no extra permissions. Criticism or voting against executives is not removal grounds. Recall needs 25% electorate petition, majority valid votes and at least half electorate participation; misconduct removal needs independent adjudication/appeal, not COO dismissal.

## Decisions and safeguards

Quorum four distinct unconflicted voters including Owner or COO and at least two elected representatives/alternates. Ordinary policy needs three yes; major structure/constitutional/OIO change needs four yes and explicit actual Owner ratification where reserved. Abstentions are not yes; no casting vote. Recusal uses elected unconflicted alternates, not discretionary executive proxies. If quorum fails, defer; no lowered denominator. Both executives conflicted means no policy adoption.

Normally consult affected functions and staff for at least 48 hours. Async deliberation is valid. Record proposal version, attendance, conflicts, named votes, reasons, effective date, implementation owner and review date; publish non-sensitive decisions. Confidential cases never go to Council for judgment or collective evidence access.

COO may impose a narrowly necessary safety/security operating measure for seven days with prompt notice, scope and expiry. No rolling reissue, case interference, constitutional amendment or representative removal through emergency policy. Longer continuation needs valid Council approval, apart from a reasoned actual Owner/platform protective obligation.

## Startup and adoption

The same five-seat design applies at 20, 30 and 60; no ED seat exists. Three elected votes provide a non-executive majority, though elections cannot guarantee independence from social pressure. Before cutover use the [current-authority reference](current-authority.md) and actual pre-reform charter or legitimately adopted interim instrument. R13 is superseded as unadopted target, not treated as a vote. R14 needs four valid affirmative votes and Owner ratification under that charter; this proposed charter cannot approve itself. If no valid quorum exists, reform remains blocked. [Ratification and migration](../proposals/v1-4-ratification.md).


## Chairing, secretariat and COO absence

COO chairs when available/unconflicted, otherwise Owner; if neither can chair procedural business, present unconflicted members select a procedural chair by majority present solely to record discussion, conflicts and deferral. This does not supply missing executive quorum, adopt policy or add a casting vote. CoS is non-voting Secretary; chair records a qualified non-voting minute-taker if absent, using existing records rather than an extra appointment grade.

After R14-S and this charter are validly ratified, an actual Owner-preauthorized acting COO whose signed instrument expressly includes the seat occupies the single COO seat during recorded activation. They must meet eligibility/conflict checks; no extra vote and no simultaneous elected seat. Any elected seat uses its elected alternate; no executive-appointed proxy. Authority ends with acting term/return. An acting appointment issued before charter adoption does not silently gain a vote. Owner seat cannot be assumed by an acting COO.

If neither Owner nor valid acting/incumbent COO can vote, quorum fails. Both personally conflicted executives cannot cure the conflict by selecting proxies. Preserve four-person quorum and three/four yes thresholds; major reserved ratification still requires the actual Owner even if an acting COO is seated. Emergency operating power is bounded, not a substitute constitutional vote.

## Example D — New policy affecting all staff

A leave-framework proposal names a Driver, consults affected staff normally 48 hours, checks conflicts, obtains valid quorum and three yes for ordinary policy, then records effective scope and implementer. An Office redesign needs four yes plus Owner ratification. CoS prepares the record but does not vote; Integration may coordinate implementation only after adoption.

## Governance Council policy proposal template

Use one proposal record to distinguish drafting, consultation, actual voting and effective scope. A blank form is never a vote, ratification or approval.

```markdown
# [Policy proposal title]
Status: Proposed — Pending Required Approval
Driver / policy owner / exact current rule / proposed wording:
Reason / alternatives / affected functions / resource and privacy impact:
Consultation dates and views / conflicts and substitutes:
Controlling charter / actual seats / quorum / named votes / reasons:
Actual Owner ratification if reserved / effective text and date:
Implementation owner / qualification-access gates / review / remaining blockers:
```

Blank templates and Git commits are not adoption evidence. [R14 package](../proposals/v1-4-ratification.md) owns transition and required approvals.
