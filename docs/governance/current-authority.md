## Pre-reform reporting and decisions

Owner supervises COO and the separate Oversight Commissioner. COO supervises active Operations and Community & Services Executive Directors plus CoS; six functional Directors report to their active portfolio or directly to COO if inactive. Operations covers T&S, Live Operations and RP; Community & Services covers Community Experience, P&C and Technology. Unit/Team management exists only for actual work. Council is policy, not a personnel superior.

Routine qualified moderation and assigned session decisions remain local; reporting is not a serial approval chain. Fill approved vacancies through receiving hiring authority and Personnel verification, not a new executive vote per applicant. Secondary assignments need home and host approval; permanent transfers need releasing and receiving Directors. Director appointments default to COO unless an actual Owner-reserved designation exists; portfolio appointment is Owner authority. Active ED resolves portfolio tradeoffs, COO cross-portfolio or inactive-portfolio deadlocks. CoS coordinates, has no line command or disciplinary authority, and does not automatically act as COO.

One permanent People Manager, voluntary accepted availability, separate task command, qualification and technical permission remain essential. Eight grades remain 01 Staff Member, 02 Moderator, 03 Senior Moderator, 04 Administrator, 05 Senior Administrator, 06 Director, 07 Executive, 08 Owner. Relevant specialists need no irrelevant moderation service; grade alone grants no case access or command.

## Current Council and adoption limits

The pre-reform five voting seats are Owner, COO, both actual Executive Directors and elected Staff Representative; CoS is non-voting Secretary and OIO advises independently. Quorum four includes Owner or COO and at least one ED; ordinary policy needs three yes, major structural/OIO changes four yes plus actual Owner ratification where reserved. Abstention is not approval. COO chairs ordinary meetings without an extra vote. The pre-reform text does not specify an automatic substitute chair; identify any actual valid local designation rather than invent one. Formal matters can be asynchronous with recorded consultation, conflicts and votes.

Inactive ED offices do not create votes. The existing charter calls for an Owner-adopted interim instrument; the [historical GOV-01 package](../proposals/v1-1-ratification.md#gov-01--interim-five-seat-council) is a drafted option, not evidence it was adopted. Until legitimate interim adoption/elections, quorum-dependent policy may be blocked. COO's genuine urgent safety operating measure lasts seven days; it cannot restructure the constitution or decide cases.

## Protected work and missing authority

OIO stays outside COO/Council command. IA fact-finding, separate initial decision and independent appeal must not be merged; protected evidence is need-to-know. Existing valid case delegations govern. Detailed new sanctions/remedies, external panels, retention and acting powers in GOV-02–04 are still pending. If no valid adjudicator/appeal mandate, safe records or qualified officer exists, record blockage, preserve safely and refer; never invent authority from a target table.

Owner appoints COO/Commissioner and retains actual ownership-level powers. A nominated successor is not an appointment. Qualified responders may use already-authorized containment; neither organizational grade nor a continuity chart bypasses Discord hierarchy or application custody. No current rule guarantees review against a hostile Owner or platform recovery without real access.
