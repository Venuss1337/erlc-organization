# Organizational ranks, eligibility and appointments

**Project version:** 1.3.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected target specification — Proposed, pending R13 ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

## Eight grades retained

| Grade | Standing and basis |
|---|---|
| 01 Staff Member | Staff appointment in any function; expert specialist may retain this standing |
| 02 Moderator | Current independently qualified enforcement appointment |
| 03 Senior Moderator | Advanced enforcement assessment and sound judgment |
| 04 Administrator | Substantive People Lead/function management or documented equivalent responsibility |
| 05 Senior Administrator | Assessed senior cross-functional responsibility, qualified Technical Lead remit or CoS minimum; not honorary expertise alone |
| 06 Director | Appointment-linked to one of three Division Heads; no permanent ED appointment |
| 07 Executive | Appointment-linked COO or Oversight Commissioner only |
| 08 Owner / Chief Executive | Actual Owner exclusively |

No compulsory sequential climb. Developers, trainers, investigators and support staff may receive 04/05 through relevant assessment and 06 through qualified Head selection without moderation experience. Qualifications and functional titles show expertise; grade does not measure personal worth or grant permissions.

## Appointment and reversion

COO appoints Heads and Technical Lead; each Head appoints working People Leads within approved capacity through published functional assessment with Personnel process verification; Owner confirms only reserved designations. Owner appoints COO and Commissioner. CoS is appointed by COO concurrently where possible; a new management office cannot be created by an acting label. Enforcement Lead requires current senior enforcement competence; other People Leads require relevant management/domain assessment and 04 minimum, granted with appointment if necessary.

One active grade per person; maintain previous validated standing separately. 06/07 end with their office and revert to retained earned grade. Reform is not misconduct: incumbents receive consultation and fair selection/reassignment, preserve qualifications and prior earned standing, and may challenge adverse process through independent Appeals under valid authority. Specialist-only assignment does not justify prestige elevation. Current qualified enforcement authorization is necessary even if 02/03 retained through explicit good-standing decision.

## Assessment and challenge

04 requires actual accountable planning, sound decisions, safe handover, integrity and fair coordination. 05 needs demonstrably senior scope and more than one substantive outcome beyond ordinary team supervision. No hours/punishment quota or arbitrary tenure. Relevant Head approves 04/05; COO decides exceptional cross-division scope and Technical Lead assessment with an unconflicted technical assessor. Personnel checks consistency, not technical judgment. Conflicted selection requires another assessor; appeal seeks fair reassessment, not automatic promotion. GOV-02 remedy powers remain pending.

[Assignments](assignments.md) preserve exactly one People Manager and scoped secondary work. [Migration](../proposals/v1-3-reform.md) owns reform appointment transitions. [Authority](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) owns approval boundaries.

## Functional eligibility

Session Host/Controller/Commander, RP coordinator/trainer, support/recruitment/personnel officer, developer/analyst, investigator/reviewer/assessor are qualification-gated 01 baseline without irrelevant moderation prerequisites. Enforcement officer is 02, senior enforcement is 03. Working enforcement People Lead needs current 03; other substantive People Leads need 04. CoS needs 05. No appointment grants unrelated permissions.
