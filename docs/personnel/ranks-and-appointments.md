## Eight grades retained

| Grade | Standing and basis |
|---|---|
| 01 Staff Member | Staff appointment in any function; expert specialist may retain this standing |
| 02 Moderator | Current independently qualified enforcement appointment |
| 03 Senior Moderator | Advanced enforcement assessment and sound judgment |
| 04 Administrator | Substantive People Lead/function management or documented equivalent responsibility |
| 05 Senior Administrator | Assessed senior cross-functional responsibility, qualified Technical Lead, Integration or CoS remit; not honorary expertise alone |
| 06 Director | Appointment-linked to one of three Division Heads; no permanent ED appointment |
| 07 Executive | Appointment-linked COO or Oversight Commissioner only |
| 08 Owner / Chief Executive | Actual Owner exclusively |

No compulsory sequential climb. Developers, trainers, investigators and support staff may receive 04/05 through relevant assessment and 06 through qualified Head selection without moderation experience. Qualifications and functional titles show expertise; grade does not measure personal worth or grant permissions.

<a id="three-rank-to-position-relationships"></a>
## Appointment and reversion

COO appoints Heads, Technical Lead and Office appointments under the [Office charter](../governance/office-of-the-coo.md#positions-and-appointment-authority); each Head appoints working People Leads within approved capacity through published functional assessment with Personnel process verification; Owner confirms only reserved designations. Owner appoints COO and Commissioner. Office concurrent holders retain one home manager; dedicated Office staff report permanently to COO. CoS is appointed by COO concurrently where possible; a new management office cannot be created by an acting label. Enforcement Lead requires current senior enforcement competence; other People Leads require relevant management/domain assessment and 04 minimum, granted with appointment if necessary.

One active grade per person; maintain previous validated standing separately. Appointment-linked 06/07 end with their respective office and revert to retained earned grade. Reform is not misconduct: incumbents receive consultation and fair selection/reassignment, preserve qualifications and prior earned standing, and may challenge adverse process through independent Appeals under valid authority. Specialist-only assignment does not justify prestige elevation. Current qualified enforcement authorization is necessary even if 02/03 retained through explicit good-standing decision.

## Assessment and challenge

04 requires actual accountable planning, sound decisions, safe handover, integrity and fair coordination. 05 needs demonstrably senior scope and more than one substantive outcome beyond ordinary team supervision. No hours/punishment quota or arbitrary tenure. Relevant Head approves 04/05; COO decides exceptional cross-division scope and Technical Lead assessment with an unconflicted technical assessor. Personnel checks consistency, not technical judgment. Conflicted selection requires another assessor; appeal seeks fair reassessment, not automatic promotion. GOV-02 remedy powers remain pending.

[Assignments](assignments.md) preserve exactly one People Manager and scoped secondary work. [Migration](../proposals/v1-4-ratification.md) owns reform appointment transitions. [Authority](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) owns approval boundaries.

## Functional eligibility

Session Host/Controller/Commander, RP coordinator/trainer, support/recruitment/personnel officer, developer/analyst, investigator/reviewer/assessor are qualification-gated 01 baseline without irrelevant moderation prerequisites. Enforcement officer is 02, senior enforcement is 03. Working enforcement People Lead needs current 03; other substantive People Leads need 04. CoS needs 05. No appointment grants unrelated permissions.

## Executive Office appointment catalogue

| Position | Grade basis | Assessor / appointor | Limits and exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Integration Director | 05 minimum, assessed senior cross-functional scope; title does not confer 06 | Relevant unconflicted Heads/Personnel assessment; COO appoints | Binding powers require separate R14-D mandate; no Head line command; retained grade basis reviewed on exit |
| CoS | 05 minimum, executive coordination assessment | COO with Personnel process check | Non-voting records/coordination, no automatic succession |
| Planning Lead | 04 substantive process responsibility; 05 only genuine assessed senior scope | Qualified analytical assessor/Personnel; COO appoints | Conditional analysis role, no command or blanket private data |
| Acting COO | 07 only during valid actual conditional appointment activation | Owner assessment, prior written appointment and candidate acceptance | Expiry returns retained standing; title alone never activates |

A concurrent Head/Integration holder displays one actual Head-linked 06 with separately recorded Integration qualification/mandate, not another grade. Exiting a 04/05 Office role prompts fair review of whether assessed earned responsibility still supports retained grade; no automatic punitive stripping or honorary retention without basis. Owner/Commissioner safeguards are unchanged.

## Functional and reserve qualifications

Functional owners and QORP reserves gain no automatic grade, permanent reports or permissions. Relevant capability is assessed for actual work; qualification-only assignments can remain 01, current enforcement keeps 02/03 requirements. Existing 04/05 management and senior adjudicator criteria are unchanged. [QORP category assessment](../oversight/qualified-reserve-pool.md#separate-capabilities-and-accountable-owners) does not qualify an initial adjudicator and vice versa.
