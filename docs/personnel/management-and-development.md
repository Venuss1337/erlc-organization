# Management review and professional development

**Project version:** 1.2.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Procedure specification; GOV-03 adoption not verified.** No new adverse-action or appeal power takes effect merely through release. [Rank definitions](ranks-and-appointments.md) remain canonical; [case adjudication](../oversight/case-handling.md) owns misconduct and independent appeal. This procedure implements volunteer-first management without a parallel promotion ladder.

## Manage performance without misclassifying misconduct

Assess the responsibilities actually accepted, safe decision quality, communication/handover, fair treatment, coaching and avoidance of unnecessary approvals. Do not assess hours online, punishment counts, popularity, medical circumstances or willingness to accept extra duties. Managers should have a realistic mandate, appropriate access and consenting coverage before being blamed for delivery failure.

Appointors review ongoing management mandates at least every six months, and after material workload/availability changes, using existing exception records and a short conversation. This is a review point, not automatic expiry of a permanent appointment or another recurring report. Acting appointments use their shorter fixed expiry. The Commissioner uses the protected GOV-02 review route; no performance review can demand a particular case outcome.

| Situation | Route | Responsible owner |
|---|---|---|
| New manager lacks experience | Coaching, mentor, observed practice and narrower decisions | Permanent line supervisor; P&C supports process |
| Repeated poor decisions, excessive reporting or missed accepted responsibilities | Documented capability/role-fit review below | Appointing line authority, using unconflicted substitute if implicated |
| Temporary unavailable volunteer or insufficient team capacity | Leave, reduced services, continuity/acting coverage | Home manager and relevant service owner |
| Alleged retaliation, corruption, discriminatory rule abuse, deliberate fabrication or serious negligent breach | IA fact-finding and separate adjudication; protective measures if necessary | OIO under case matrix; routine coaching cannot dispose of serious allegations |

## Proportionate review and reassignment

1. **Understand:** Supervisor privately gives concrete examples and the relevant accepted expectation, asks for response, and checks training, scope, workload and conflicting instructions. P&C can supply an alternative unconflicted contact. Record a short agreed summary, not private diagnoses.
2. **Support:** Agree two or three observable improvements, training/mentor, decisions to delegate, reduced duties if wanted, evidence to review and a realistic review point—normally two to four weeks of agreed opportunities, extended for leave. No compulsory shifts, unpaid hours quota or guaranteed promotion.
3. **Review:** Supervisor considers actual opportunities and the volunteer's response. Close if resolved; adapt support if realistic; otherwise recommend smaller remit, lateral transfer or removal from the management appointment. Missing staffing or permissions should result in service reduction/escalation, not a fabricated personal failure.
4. **Decide:** The **original appointing authority** decides appointment removal/reassignment: Directorate Director for team/unit appointments; COO for Directorate Director; Owner for Executive Director, COO or Commissioner; COO for Chief of Staff. OIO functional appointments follow the protected Commissioner process and external review where conflicted. Give written reasons and normally seven days to respond before an adverse decision. A conflicted authority uses the case-procedure external route; no subordinate quietly adjudicates their superior.
5. **Challenge:** An affected volunteer has the independent 14-day appeal route in the case procedure once GOV-02 is active. Pending activation, offer independent advisory procedural review and record any remedy not delegated. Popularity does not block review; an appeal does not automatically entitle someone to continue an unsafe managerial role.
6. **Close and hand over:** Record retained grade separately from appointment-linked reversion, non-punitive transfer/leave options, limited access changes and continuity owner. Do not label administrative reassignment as misconduct. Removal from an appointment does not itself dismiss someone from staff or automatically remove a valid earned grade.

If protective restriction is necessary, give the risk reason and use the case procedure's independent review and expiry safeguards. Repeat management concerns involving the COO or Commissioner use Owner decision plus external review; Owner-related concerns have only the explicitly limited advisory panel route.

## Fair selection and assessment

For each vacancy publish the work, assessment criteria, access limits and real appointor before selection. Use a work sample plus structured questions and record evidence against the same criteria for all candidates. A declared friend, recent direct collaborator with a personal stake, or person who preselected the winner must not be sole assessor; use an unconflicted assessor from another function or screened external assessor where necessary. P&C verifies the process, not the substantive technical judgment by itself. Give applicants a concise reason and independent challenge route; do not disclose another applicant's private record.

Relevant specialist experience can demonstrate leadership and organizational judgment. No requirement to serve in moderation first, and no arbitrary wait at every grade. Combined appointment/grade decisions avoid a separate promotion queue. Any stricter functional criterion must be job-relevant, published and consistent with the [eligibility framework](ranks-and-appointments.md#position-and-eligibility-matrix--all-six-directorates-and-oversight).

## Expertise recognition without another hierarchy

Use existing functional titles, current qualifications, credited mentoring and a consented contribution note: for example `Systems Developer · Staff Member (01) · qualified recovery maintainer`. Record competence, scope, evidence and refresher/review date; show training state where needed. No universal Expert rank, numbered specialist levels, honorary grade 04/05, command over unrelated staff or permissions by badge. Specialists may decline management without losing recognition or access needed for authorized work. A proposed recognition scheme that changes standing or command must return to Council rather than quietly become another ladder.
