## Migration and single cutover

1. Verify actual controlling instruments, existing offices, delegations, earned grades/qualifications, access, accepted duties and case mandates in restricted records; no completed private record in Git.
2. Consult affected volunteers. Map every function under the [ownership map](../operations/function-map.md). Fairly select/confirm Heads and Technical Lead; no misconduct label or unjustified loss of earned standing for structural reassignment. Former portfolio/unit appointments receive consent-based delivery, coordinator or People Lead options, response and independent challenge.
3. Assess Office appointees for real work and capacity; record one home manager, primary/secondary allocation, qualified domain decisions and displaced delivery. Do not create four dedicated executives at 20. Owner separately signs tested conditional succession only after lawful authority is clear.
4. Approve senior pool eligibility and assign separated investigators/adjudicators/reviewers; no case-role inheritance. If no safe independent route exists, preserve/referral and blocked status rather than a fictitious case service.
5. Hold legitimate target elections, terms and consenting alternates under approved instrument; protect nonexecutive representation. Establish chair/minute coverage and explicit acting-seat exclusions.
6. At ONE recorded effective cutover, issue new mandates, revoke retired appointment powers/obsolete roles, notify staff of actual owners and update current-authority status. Before that instant old rules control; afterwards adopted target scope controls. Preserve case records and valid mandates; re-screen reporting conflicts.
7. Test least privilege, actual application/Owner custody, restricted personnel/OIO storage, safe duty windows and qualified backups. Launch only services covered by accepted capability. No handbook can transfer platform ownership or compel hostile Owner compliance.
8. Review after 30 and 90 days using actual exception work, missed accepted commitments, Head/COO overload, case independence, delegated decision quality, private-data protection and service reductions. Modify through valid authority, not silent executive title restoration.
