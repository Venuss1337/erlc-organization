# R13 structural amendment and migration

**Project version:** 1.3.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Proposed — Pending Required Approval.** Driver: COO or named unconflicted sponsor when appointed; policy owner: Owner/Council, consulting Heads, Personnel and all protected OIO functions. No actual vote, officer acceptance or cutover is evidenced.

## Adoption instrument

Adopt the target [structure](../governance/structure.md), [Council charter](../governance/governance-council.md), [function ownership](../operations/function-map.md), [rank mapping](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md) and [decision matrix](../operations/authority-and-escalation.md) as one coherent R13 amendment. Three divisions and a compact Office replace six directorates; permanent ED and Unit Manager offices end. The same five Council seats operate at every staffing size. The narrower intra-division transfer route replaces two former Director approvals with one Head approval after consultation; across divisions two approvals remain.

Require four affirmative votes from a valid pre-reform Council and explicit actual Owner ratification, with normally at least 48 hours' affected-staff consultation and independent OIO advice. Record actual charter used, officeholders/elected substitutes, conflicts, votes, minutes, approved revision, effective date, transition owner and 90-day review. If no EDs/quorum exist, the old charter's express interim power must first be exercised through a real adopted instrument and elections. The proposed target Council cannot approve itself. Until then routine old delegations continue, reform adoption is blocked, and seven-day urgent safety measures cannot restructure the constitution.

GOV-01's earlier ED-dependent interim proposal is superseded as a target, not falsely recorded as previously approved. GOV-02 case powers and external reserves remain pending; R13 can adopt their preserved protections only through explicit inclusion in the actual vote/ratification instrument. GOV-03/04 operational provisions likewise need explicit effective scope; no silent bundle approval. Case safeguards are not weakened while awaiting adoption. Technical access/recovery tests and safe records remain implementation gates.

## Personnel migration

1. Inventory actual appointments, retained grades, qualifications, restricted access, accepted duties and ongoing cases. No confidential records in Git.
2. Consult each affected volunteer; map every function under the published ownership map. Select three Heads fairly through work-relevant assessment and conflict checks; no incumbent entitlement or assumed demotion for misconduct.
3. Offer former EDs/Directors delivery, People Lead, coordinator or specialist assignments matching consent and capacity. Appointment-linked 06/07 ends only on actual office exit; preserve validated retained standing and qualifications. Record reasons, response opportunity and independent challenge; do not strip earned 04/05 simply to fit the chart.
4. Appoint Technical Lead through relevant technical/senior responsibility assessment; prior moderation experience is irrelevant. CoS may be held concurrently by a qualified existing contributor, retaining one home manager; its coordination appointment reports to COO without changing the home reporting record.
5. Name exactly one new permanent People Manager and primary assignment per person. Host/project command is separate. Agree changed secondary allocations; do not duplicate managers during transition.
6. At a single recorded cutover, revoke retired appointment delegations/access, issue new scoped mandates and notify teams. Before that instant old authority governs; after it target authority governs. Do not run parallel competing executive chains. Preserve valid case mandates, evidence custody and independent appeal separation; re-screen changed reporting conflicts.
7. Elect/seat target Council under the ratified instrument, including alternates and different-area eligibility. Publish safe transition summary. Verify actual coverage, restricted stores, custody, access removal and recovery; launch only safe services.
8. Review after 30 and 90 days: missed accepted duties, Head/COO overload, privacy failures, independent-review capacity and unnecessary escalation. If capacity fails, reduce service first; propose adjustment rather than quietly restore retired offices.
