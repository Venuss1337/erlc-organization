# Authority, decision ownership and escalation

**Project version:** 1.3.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected target specification — Proposed, pending R13 ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

Rank, appointment, qualification, operational mandate, governance decision and technical permission remain distinct. Routine work has one authorized decision owner; reporting is not an approval queue.

| Decision | Target owner | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Routine moderation | Qualified authorized officer | Published enforcement scope, evidence/reasons, independent appeal |
| Permanent ban | Specifically authorized T&S role | No automatic authority by grade or session command |
| Session start/end | Qualified assigned Commander | Safe coverage and accepted window |
| RP program decision | Qualified Program Commander within Game division | RP standards, no general moderation powers |
| Fill approved vacancy | Receiving functional hiring authority | Personnel verification and training; no new executive vacancy approval per candidate |
| Secondary assignment | Home People Manager + host lead | Both consent, one allocation record |
| Home transfer within same division | Division Head after home/receiving manager consultation | One substantive approval; qualification/capacity/privacy checks retained |
| Home transfer across divisions | Releasing + receiving Heads (Technical Lead for Office) | Two approvals, Personnel records; COO only deadlock |
| Grade 02/03 | T&S Head or written qualified delegate | Current enforcement assessment; no implicit delegation |
| Grade 04/05 | Relevant Head; COO for Office/exceptional scope | Evidence-backed assessment and Personnel check |
| Head / Technical Lead appointment | COO | Fair selection; Owner only existing reserved designation |
| Working People Lead appointment | Relevant Head | Approved capacity, relevant qualification and fair selection |
| Cross-function conflict in one division | Its Head | No former-directorate negotiation stage |
| Cross-division conflict | COO | No portfolio relay |
| IA / initial discipline / appeal | Protected case function and separate authorized adjudicator | Case procedure; no self-review |
| Privileged access/restoration | Authorized approver then separate qualified executor | Technical Lead cannot self-approve; COO/Owner substitutes as required; case owner controls case visibility |
| Organization-wide policy | Valid Council | Three yes ordinary; four plus Owner where reserved |

For ordinary staff and working People Leads, initial disciplinary adjudicator is unconflicted Division Head, or COO for Technical Office; another unconflicted Head/external adjudicator replaces a conflicted authority under GOV-02. Heads and Technical Lead are adjudicated by COO, with external appeal; COO by actual Owner with external investigation/appeal. OIO personnel and Commissioner use protected external routes. Owner allegations retain independent advisory limitations. No Council individual-case judgment.

The case procedure's separation, notice/response, proof, graduated sanctions, appeal, evidence and recusal rules remain. Any row retaining a pre-reform label is read only through this explicit target mapping; no removed office acquires residual powers. Adoption is required before the new intra-division single transfer approval can operate.
