# Operating practices, capacity and staffing

**Project version:** 1.3.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected target specification — Proposed, pending R13 ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

Use accepted voluntary availability, not roster size. No mandatory weekly hours, punishment quotas or automatic misconduct for reduced activity. Leads arrange handoffs or reduce service. Each live session needs a qualified Commander and a different qualified enforcement responder plus real access; no double coverage of simultaneous sessions. No independent review without unconflicted officers, separate adjudication/appeal and safe records. Optional events/changes pause before critical safety work.

One shared action/exception entry per operational question: owner, next action, review date, capacity/risk and decision reference. Weekly checks examine changed exceptions; monthly reviews use existing evidence asynchronously where possible. No duplicate CoS/Head/COO reports. Council notice/votes and restricted case records remain separate. Optional wellbeing check-ins, leave, pulse and exit feedback remain voluntary and confidential. Player consultations and public improvement updates remain Community functions with private complaints excluded.

At 20: three working Heads, Technical Lead, COO and Commissioner directly manage actual staff; no People Leads or dedicated CoS required. At 30: one working Game People Lead only where coaching work justifies it. At 60: two working Leads per large division; none is a Unit Manager above another Lead. [Roster and measured spans](../reform-evidence.md) show actual unique people. Capability gaps trigger fewer sessions, slower nonurgent support, paused optional events and screened case alternates, never compelled work.

Review Head workload monthly using exceptions, accepted delivery and coaching quality. If Game program/session conflicts or Community privacy/people work exceed capacity, first narrow commitments and delegate concrete functions. Adding a permanent layer requires a new reasoned governance proposal; no title automatically appears at a headcount threshold. Acting coverage follows [continuity](continuity.md), not highest rank.
