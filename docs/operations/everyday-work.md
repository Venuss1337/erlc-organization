# Everyday staff tasks

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Existing case separation, confidentiality, volunteer consent and technical approval controls remain; no cutover is evidenced.

**Instructional guide under existing authority, not a new policy.** Start at your task, contact the named owner and keep the required record once. Canonical links control exceptions and full procedures. New v1.1 procedures remain approval-dependent; this guide does not activate them. Actual team contacts, qualifications and enforcement/session playbooks must be supplied by the organization.

## Join organizational staff

Apply for a real vacancy through Talent Acquisition. The receiving team assesses relevant work, its authorized hiring manager selects, and P&C checks the process and records one home team/manager. Training and approved access follow. A preapproved vacancy does not need another executive vacancy vote for each applicant; creating capacity is a separate decision. Do not post applications or personal details in this repository.

**Owner:** receiving hiring authority. **Record:** existing vacancy/application and personnel entry; link existing evidence. [Hiring workflow](../personnel/recruitment.md#hiring-workflow).

## Complete initial training

Ask the receiving lead/Learning & Development for your role's assessment and supervised work. Complete that qualification, then confirm the authorized assignment/access before independent duty. Course completion alone does not grant moderation or privileged permissions. If no role-specific standard or assessor exists, ask the Division Head/P&C; remain supervised or pause that work.

**Owner:** relevant qualification assessor/receiving team. **Record:** qualification result in existing personnel entry. [Recruitment qualification/onboarding](../personnel/recruitment.md#hiring-workflow).

## Begin an assigned duty

Check the accepted window, task lead, current qualification, necessary access and handoff/end contact. Begin work already within your assignment without climbing the reporting chart. Report an unsafe gap to the duty lead instead of assuming permission. Role names are not confirmation that someone is available.

**Owner:** assigned task lead; you act within accepted scope. **Record:** existing duty roster/outcome, not a second permission form. [Assignment responsibilities](../personnel/assignments.md#who-is-responsible-for-what).

## Issue a routine moderation action

Check the applicable rule, evidence/context, your enforcement authorization and permitted action. Take the delegated action, record concise reasons/evidence and provide the published appeal route. No separate Division Head/ED/COO approval for an action already within your remit. If the action exceeds your delegation or platform permission, refer to the authorized T&S role; do not improvise a sanction or use rank to bypass technical limits.

**Owner:** qualified authorized Moderator/Officer. **Record:** enforcement case note. [Decision matrix](authority-and-escalation.md#delegated-decision-matrix). A local enforcement playbook is still required; this guide is not an offence/penalty list.

## Escalate a serious violation

Immediate risk: alert the qualified responder/command contact and take only already-authorized containment. Preserve safe relevant identifiers/context when it will not delay protection. Lasting player sanctions go to authorized T&S; suspected staff abuse goes to OIO/IA; platform danger may be reported directly. These questions may have linked records, not competing full investigations.

**Owner:** qualified domain responder, then relevant case authority. **Record:** incident/enforcement reference with limited sharing. [Incident boundaries](incident-response.md#powers-and-boundaries) · [Report safely](../oversight/get-help.md#report-staff-misconduct).

## Host a scheduled session

Confirm the assigned qualified Commander, available enforcement support, hosting/control access and safe session plan. Hosts/controllers prepare and conduct assigned tasks; Commander starts/ends their session under published standards. Refer lasting enforcement to T&S. If coverage fails, tell Commander/service lead and pause/end safely. The proposed v1.1 coverage check needs a separate Commander and enforcement responder; do not claim that rule is ratified here.

**Owner:** assigned qualified Commander; no extra executive launch approval for an already-assigned session. **Record:** roster and concise outcome. [Live Operations mandate](../directorates/live-operations.md) · [Coverage specification](operating-practices.md#capacity-and-service-levels).

## Handle a member support request

Answer within Member Services' scope and existing instructions. Keep one ticket reference. Route only the specialist question you cannot resolve, telling the member who owns it next; do not make them obtain managerial permission to submit a complaint/appeal. Share only necessary details. Public suggestions go to Player Voice; private evidence never goes into the public tracker.

**Owner:** assigned Support Officer within remit; specialist owner after referral. **Record:** ticket and scoped referral. [Community Experience mandate](../directorates/community-experience.md).

## Request leave or reduced availability

Tell your Home People Manager what availability/duties you wish to change; no diagnosis or personal explanation is required. If an accepted live duty is affected, notify its task lead when able so they can arrange handoff or cancel service. P&C can provide an unconflicted support contact. Reduced activity is not automatically misconduct.

**Owner:** home manager for support/allocation; service lead for coverage. **Record:** minimum agreed availability action in the existing assignment record. [Staff Experience safeguards](../directorates/people-and-culture.md#staff-experience--retention-framework).

## Request a secondary assignment

Send the proposed role/window to your Home People Manager and receiving lead. They check capability, conflicts and voluntary capacity and both approve the scope; Personnel records the existing allocation agreement. This is two required approvals, not a single-lead shortcut. Qualification/access follows through its separate authorized route. Do not resign from your primary team merely to apply.

**Owners:** home manager + host lead. **Record:** one allocation agreement, linked to the existing personnel entry. [Secondary assignment workflow](../personnel/assignments.md#secondary-assignment-workflow).

## Change an accepted duty

Tell the current task lead what window you can no longer cover and any safe handoff information. The lead arranges qualified coverage, reschedules or cancels; inform your home manager if the approved allocation changes. No executive approval for an ordinary shift adjustment within the lead's mandate. Changing a standing cross-team allocation still needs home/host approval; new privileges need their own approval.

**Owner:** duty/task lead within approved assignment. **Record:** update the existing roster/allocation. [Accepted-duty and allocation rules](../personnel/assignments.md#priorities-when-assignments-collide).

## Report staff misconduct

Use the confidential OIO route or protected lead; a manager's permission is unnecessary. Send a safe contact, account IDs, what happened and relevant dates/links. Do not investigate it yourself or circulate private evidence. See [reporting and conflict options](../oversight/get-help.md#report-staff-misconduct).

**Owner after submission:** unconflicted OIO intake/IA. **Record:** protected intake, outside Git/public channels.

## Appeal an enforcement or personnel decision

Send the decision/reference, receipt date, disputed error/grounds, relevant material and requested correction to OIO Appeals. Do not ask the original actor to authorize your appeal. The new specification normally allows 14 days, with reasonable late-submission exceptions, but GOV-02 adoption and delegated remedies remain unverified. Use any valid published local appeal terms and ask OIO to identify them; a pending package must not be presented as binding.

**Owner:** uninvolved authorized Appeals reviewer. **Record:** independent appeal linked to original decision. [Appeal guidance](../oversight/get-help.md#request-an-appeal).

## Apply for promotion or a leadership vacancy

Ask for the published responsibility, relevant qualification and appointor; provide work evidence against those criteria. Moderator grades use T&S assessment/approval. Grade 04/05 uses relevant Division Head and P&C process check, with COO review for exceptional scope. Division Head standing follows actual appointment; technical candidates do not need moderation experience. A real appointment and its grade can be decided together; no extra prestige-promotion committee.

**Owner:** specified grade/appointment authority. **Record:** existing assessment/appointment and personnel entry. [Rank progression](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#promotions-appointments-transfers-and-reversions). Detailed v1.1 assessment clarifications remain GOV-03 proposals; challenge unfair process through independent Appeals.

## Transfer between home teams

Within one division ask its Head after both home managers are consulted; across divisions/Technology ask both releasing and receiving owners, with qualifications, capacity, new home manager and handover identified. Workforce facilitates and Personnel records; neither is a third appointor. A blocked transfer uses the named valid Integration/domain mandate; reserved questions go to COO/valid acting COO. Do not silently change home team or assume a temporary host lead became your permanent manager.

**Owners:** one Head within division; releasing + receiving owners across divisions. **Record:** existing transfer/personnel entry. [Assignment authority](../personnel/assignments.md#who-is-responsible-for-what).

## Resolve conflicting assignments

Tell both task leads about the clash. Use the documented priority: genuine scoped emergency, previously accepted live duty, approved primary, approved secondary, optional work. Leads negotiate first; unresolved conflict goes to the owning Head or named valid domain/Integration mandate; only reserved exceptions go to COO/valid acting COO. Use a valid named domain/Integration alternate if an Office appointment is inactive; no invented authority. This is an exception route, not required approval from every level for an agreed adjustment.

**Owner:** relevant leads within allocations; escalation owner only if unresolved. **Record:** one roster/allocation update. [Priority rule](../personnel/assignments.md#priorities-when-assignments-collide).

## Handle an unavailable supervisor

Check for an already-valid named alternate/delegation; otherwise contact the next **active** line authority. Existing routine work within your own mandate continues. A missing supervisor does not create self-promotion or permission to perform their reserved decisions. If nobody can validly authorize consequential work, defer it or reduce service; qualified emergency protection remains available within its scope. OIO uses independent case alternates, not ordinary executive substitution.

**Owner:** next valid appointing/line authority. **Record:** existing exception/acting mandate if needed. [Continuity matrix](continuity.md#who-may-designate-coverage) is a GOV-04 specification; no automatic acting authority is granted by this guide.

## Target transfer rule

Within one merged division, the Head approves a home transfer after both managers are consulted. Across divisions or Technology Office, releasing and receiving owners both approve. This R14 change is inactive before ratified cutover; old two-Director approval remains in force until then.

## Office decisions without another approval chain

If a recorded Integration mandate covers the unresolved resource question, its unconflicted holder decides after consultation; do not seek routine COO reapproval. Heads still report directly to COO. CoS can request minimum status from an existing commitment record, not command work or demand private evidence. An acting COO requires prior actual authorization and accepted activation; no Office title is automatic succession. Before ratified cutover use the [current-authority reference](../governance/current-authority.md).

## Accept functional or reserve work

Check relevant qualification, actual mandate, conflicts and compatible windows with the existing home/host or protected appointor. Record which prior duties stop before accepting. Functional ownership does not change your permanent manager. QORP needs separate category assessment, consent, case mandate and safe access; no reserve is automatically on call. Use [capacity](management-capacity.md) and [reserve activation](../oversight/qualified-reserve-pool.md#case-activation-and-independence).
