# Temporary authority and continuity

**Project version:** 1.4.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Existing case separation, confidentiality, volunteer consent and technical approval controls remain; no cutover is evidenced.

**Procedure specification; GOV-04 adoption not verified.** This page owns acting mandates and handover, not new permanent deputy positions. Reserved appointments require actual Owner authorization. Protected case work follows the [case alternate procedure](../oversight/case-handling.md#alternates-and-external-reviewers), not a general acting executive.

## Who may designate coverage

| Absent role | Designating authority | Qualified coverage and limits |
|---|---|---|
| COO | Actual Owner issues conditional appointment BEFORE absence, or makes an actual appointment while available | Only named qualified consenting primary/reserve may activate accepted scope; no title-based inheritance. R14-S and Council rules below control seat, expiry and exclusions. |
| Division Head | COO, with Owner confirmation only where a published reserved designation exists | Qualified manager/specialist assessed for remit, combined acting appointment/grade 06 permitted; no irrelevant moderator prerequisite. If no suitable person, COO retains essential decisions and reduces service. |
| Team Lead / working People Lead | Division Head | Competent consenting colleague meeting job-specific qualifications and grade floor through combined assessment where necessary; only specified team. |
| Critical technical maintainer | Technical Lead authorizes operational scope; access approved under technical policy | Prequalified backup with tested recovery capability and named access; no shared personal login or automatic secret inheritance. If no recovery access, contain through available paths and keep system offline. |
| Oversight Commissioner | Actual Owner, after unconflicted protected-lead advice | Qualified unconflicted administrative acting head; no power over findings. Lead-specific case officers continue existing case mandates; no successor blanket file access. |
| IA / Appeals / Assurance officer | Unconflicted Commissioner; special/external appointor for leadership conflicts | Qualified unconflicted alternate under case procedure; separate investigation, adjudication and appeal. Owner panel selection remains separate. |
| Technical Lead officeholder | COO or valid acting COO; preauthorized named technical alternate may activate existing scope | Qualified technical/leadership assessment, grade 05, separate privilege approval; no assumed token/application custody or self-approved restoration. |
| Integration Director | COO for appointment; existing mandate names qualified conflict/absence alternate | Domain owners retain ordinary work; no CoS inheritance. Alternate cannot exceed same delegated envelope. |
| Planning Lead | COO or preauthorized planning process substitute | Existing service owners read their own capacity records; no new decision or case access. |
| Chief of Staff / Council Secretary | COO/valid acting for coordination; valid Council procedural chair records minute-taker | Existing qualified contributor keeps records only; no automatic COO mandate or voting seat. |

An officeholder may nominate coverage in advance; nomination alone is not appointment. Designating authority can preauthorize a named, qualified alternate for a stated absence window with conditional activation and acceptance. No self-appointment by seniority. An unavailable appointor means use an already-valid written delegation, existing superior's ordinary authority or reduced service—not an invented succession chain. Owner absence cannot be solved by pretending ownership transfers.

## Prepared COO succession

**Proposed R14-S; requires actual Owner authorization under the controlling charter before activation.** Owner evaluates at least a primary and reserve where feasible with unconflicted management/Personnel advice: operational judgment across all four delivery functions, fair delegation, scope limits, privacy, incident coordination, conflict handling, usable records/access and a tabletop exercise. Obtain each person's written consent, accepted availability, displaced-duty plan and one permanent home manager. If only one qualifies, state single-candidate risk; do not invent a reserve. Integration, CoS or a Head may qualify, never by title alone.

Owner signs a conditional acting appointment naming the ordered candidates, exact powers/exclusions, activation evidence, notification contacts, grade/reversion, access, initial end date and maximum duration. The standing authorization expires within 90 days unless actually renewed after checks; it cannot authorize activation after its expiry. A live activation ends on its stated date, no later than 30 days after activation or the standing authorization's expiry, whichever comes first. Owner may expressly renew an active appointment once for up to 30 days after capacity/conflict review; no unavailable-person auto-renewal.

| Absence category | Activation and action |
|---|---|
| Planned short absence | COO gives handover; named candidate confirms availability; conditional appointment activates on recorded start |
| Unexpected unavailable | At the instrument's agreed response window, CoS or named records alternate makes two safe contact attempts over the next 12 hours and records no response; named candidate confirms availability and no conflict, then activates the ALREADY-SIGNED appointment. This is evidence of a trigger, not a new appointment. Immediate domain protection never waits 12 hours |
| Credible incapacity / resignation | Record verified notice and inform actual Owner; named candidate can activate existing signed trigger immediately if it expressly covers this event. Otherwise only actual Owner appoints; ordinary mandates continue |
| COO and Owner unavailable | Same preexisting appointment can activate within validity. No new appointment, extension, ownership transfer or reserved Owner decision can be invented |
| Primary unavailable/conflicted | Recorded consenting reserve acts only if preauthorized; never two acting COOs. If both unavailable, reduce services and defer executive-only work |

A concurrent acting candidate suspends or reallocates their original duty/Integration arbitration within the SAME accepted capacity budget. Named valid alternates cover those functions; if none, reduce/defer services. Do not count the same hours as both Integration and acting COO capacity.

Use a shared restricted activation record: authority reference, trigger/contact evidence, candidate capability/availability/conflicts, acceptance, scope, start/end, notified Heads/Owner/Personnel, access approval and displaced work. A second unconflicted existing records contact checks identity, trigger and dates when available; this is verification/notification, not a discretionary new appointment or emergency veto. If evidence or identity is uncertain, do not activate contested authority. Technical permissions remain separately tested. Publish a safe notice of current acting authority, not private reasons.

Acting scope may include ordinary COO integration, specified appointments, management review and seven-day safety measures only when the instrument explicitly grants them. Exclude Owner-reserved powers, OIO findings/case access, self-appointment, constitutional adoption by fiat, alteration of the succession instrument and self-approved privileges. Personal case recusal uses the adjudication route, not a proxy selected by the subject. A ratified Council provision may allow the single COO seat; operational appointment alone never does.

On return, verify the actual incumbent's identity and return notice. Where the prior instrument provides for resumption, record handover/resumption without needing a fresh unavailable Owner signature; otherwise original appointor records it. Close temporary grade/access and restore retained standing; preserve valid decisions. Resignation triggers normal Owner-led permanent selection during the acting term. If expiry arrives without lawful extension/replacement, executive authority ends: keep local mandates and safe reduced service, not endless acting government.

## Record, activate and expire

Use the existing [appointment template](../personnel/ranks-and-appointments.md#management-appointment-template), with this minimum record: absent office, reason category (no private explanation), appointor, consenting replacement, capability/conflict check, one permanent home People Manager, start/end, authorized decisions, excluded reserved/case powers, temporary grade and prior retained grade, access approver/executor, handover contact and return check.

Default acting term is **up to 30 calendar days**. Appointor may renew once up to 30 days after checking capacity, conflicts and performance; a longer absence needs a normal selection/appointment review or a published reduced-service arrangement, not endless automatic renewals. A case officer's access expiry follows the case procedure independently; a constitutional emergency measure still expires under the Council charter, not the acting term.

Notify affected staff and Personnel before activation when foreseeable; unexpected loss requires prompt notice after a valid acting decision. Technology grants only necessary time-limited permissions, records expiry and verifies revocation at expiry/return. If the platform cannot auto-expire a role, a named custodian schedules and confirms manual removal; no claim of automatic enforcement. Never give the acting officer broad case access as a convenience.

Outgoing handover, if available: unresolved decisions, accepted duties, safe contact/records locations, live risks, current restrictions, next review and access inventory. Do not transmit secrets in the handover note. Unexpected resignation: supervisor reconstructs from shared work records, protects relevant evidence, revokes access promptly and contacts consenting backups. Respect that a departing volunteer may be unable to provide more work.

At return, original appointing authority records resumption, briefs the returning holder, closes pending delegations and confirms removal of temporary grade/access. Acting decisions validly taken remain recorded; original holder does not secretly erase them. Personnel checks reversion to retained standing; an adverse misconduct finding requires the separate case procedure. The acting role does not create a second permanent People Manager.

## Continuity readiness check

Division Heads review single-person dependencies when staffing changes and during monthly exception review: critical task, primary, consenting qualified backup, real access/recovery path, last exercise date, safe degraded mode. OIO separately checks independent case alternates. Mark each **untested**, **tested with date/evidence**, or **unavailable**. No completed confidential register belongs in Git. Absence of a backup is a service constraint, not a volunteer's moral failure.
