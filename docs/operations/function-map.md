# Complete function ownership and consolidation map

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**Selected R14 target specification — Proposed, pending ratification.** Until a recorded cutover, the pre-reform charter remains controlling; no vote or appointment is claimed.

Every original function below has one target accountable owner. A coordinator is a working assignment, not a replacement Director. Qualifications are retained and refreshed for relevant scope; no unrelated moderation prerequisite.

| Former home | Functions transferred intact | Accountable owner / working ownership | Boundaries and dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trust & Safety | Discord moderation; game enforcement; case/evidence quality; critical incident rotation | T&S Head; qualified moderation/enforcement leads, evidence coordinator, duty IC | No independent IA/appeal authority; coordinate session logistics with Game |
| Live Operations | Scheduling/dispatch; hosts/controllers/session command; session quality/readiness | Game Head; dispatch coordinator, operational Commanders, readiness coach | No permanent global bans; T&S qualified enforcement; Tech access |
| Roleplay Programs | Law Enforcement, Fire/EMS, DOT, Civilian; program training/evaluation, uniforms/vehicles, membership/promotions | Game Head; working Program Commanders/coordinators and RP standards assessor | RP players not staff; no general Discord authority; qualification records with People |
| Community Experience | Member services/onboarding; engagement/events/comms; player feedback/consultations/tracker; partnerships/growth | Community & People Head; support/event/feedback/partnership coordinators | Confidential complaints to OIO, no public personnel details |
| People & Culture | Recruitment/selection administration; learning/assessment; personnel/records; workforce allocation; retention/leave/check-ins | Community & People Head; qualified recruitment/training/personnel/retention owners | Hiring authority selects, personnel verifies; restricted personnel storage separate from support; one home manager |
| Technology & Systems | Engineering/bots/integrations; infrastructure/access/recovery; data/reporting | Technical Lead; qualified developers, access/recovery custodian, analyst | Privilege approval separate from execution, Owner native control, OIO independent assurance |
| OIO (unchanged) | Shared intake; IA; independent Appeals; systemic Assurance | Commissioner administers; separate assigned investigator, adjudicator, appeal reviewer and assessor | No self-review, no operating command; external reserves if conflict/capacity failure |

Former portfolio integration work is distributed among accountable Heads, named domain owners and scoped Integration mandates under the [Office charter](../governance/office-of-the-coo.md); COO retains reserved exceptions. No retired portfolio office retains powers. Six Directorate Director offices become three Heads and Technical Lead through fair selection, not automatic incumbent assignment. Unit Manager layer is removed; its coverage planning goes to dispatch/coordinators and its coaching to working People Leads. Session/program command stays operational. CoS's minutes/action tracking is concurrent or dedicated only after explicit workload allocation and carries no extra vote.

## Updated worked examples

- A: An enforcement officer hosting a session keeps one Safety People Manager; the assigned Commander directs accepted session tasks only.
- B: A solo Partnerships coordinator reports to the Community Head/recorded People Lead without an empty manager/deputy hierarchy.
- C: A Game-only session procedure is decided by its Head; a cross-organization ban-policy change goes to Council.
- D: A leave-framework amendment needs valid Council approval; individual availability remains voluntary.
- E: A complaint against a Head goes to independent IA, separate assigned unconflicted senior-pool adjudication and uninvolved appeal, never the Head's own review.
- F: A new session squad needs accepted qualified coverage and approved capacity; its Commander is not automatically a People Manager.
- G: A contested ban and alleged staff abuse receive linked, distinct Appeals and IA questions, not duplicate full investigations.
- H: A burned-out volunteer may pause work without diagnosis disclosure; leads hand off or reduce service.
- I: Player feedback gets one delivery owner and a public non-sensitive improvement update; private cases remain outside that tracker.

## Executive work ownership

COO owns operating direction and reserved executive decisions; Integration owns only recorded cross-division residual domains; CoS owns executive/Council records and follow-through; conditional Planning owns aggregate capacity analysis. The [Office charter](../governance/office-of-the-coo.md) is canonical. No new delivery division, Office vote or intermediary personnel chain is created.

## R15 working ownership

Routine execution follows the [functional responsibility matrix](management-capacity.md#binding-functional-responsibility-matrix); Heads remain accountable, People Leads coach named reports, and operational owners do not become another permanent management chain. QORP capabilities remain under [protected oversight](../oversight/qualified-reserve-pool.md), separate from initial adjudicators.
