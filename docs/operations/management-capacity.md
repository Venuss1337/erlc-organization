# Management capacity and functional ownership

**Project version:** 1.5.0 · **Release date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** Official release.

**R15-M specification — Proposed, pending required approval.** This refines the R14 target without changing executive architecture. [Current authority](../governance/current-authority.md) controls until actual adoption/cutover. No hours below are mandatory volunteer commitments.

## Three responsibilities, one permanent manager

| Appointment | Owns and can decide | Does not automatically own |
|---|---|---|
| Division Head | Direction, service envelope, managerial supervision, standards, consequential personnel decisions and exception accountability | Every roster edit, routine program task, ticket or training record |
| Working People Lead | Defined direct-report group, coaching, feedback, accepted allocations, development and ordinary personnel support within appointment | Unlimited delivery, independent discipline, another team's permanent supervision |
| Functional / Operational Lead | Named service decisions within published standards, accepted capacity and qualifications; directs accepted task work | Permanent reports, grades, leave approval, appointment removal, privileged access or protected case decisions |

Heads appoint qualified functional owners within approved capacity after a short work-relevant assessment and consent. Record scope, actual tasks, limits, qualified backup, expiry/review and displaced delivery in the EXISTING assignment/mandate record. Functional appointments are working assignments, not new permanent managerial offices or automatic rank changes. Relevant qualification-only roles can remain grade 01; current enforcement still requires its qualification/grade. A substantive management-grade assessment follows the existing ranks policy, not a badge upgrade.

The permanent People Manager remains unchanged. A functional owner can direct accepted work within scope; a People Lead handles career/availability concerns. One person may combine functions only with a single total capacity budget and explicit conflicts. No mandatory second signoff for an already-delegated routine decision. Head nondelegation despite safe qualified capacity uses the capability-review procedure, not automatic IA.

At 20, existing qualified contributors combine session/RP administration in narrow windows; no new permanent manager. One person cannot simultaneously command two sessions. At 30, session/RP functions are normally separated among existing contributors, one Game People Lead retained only if coaching load justifies it. At 60, distinct working session/RP owners plus program coordinators handle delivery; two Game People Leads retain their reporting groups but receive reduced frontline commitments. No Session Manager or RP Director layer is introduced.

## Whole-person capacity and assignments

Use one existing assignment entry per person: accepted windows, qualifications, primary delivery, permanent management, functional work, Office work, casework, training, administration, incident work and necessary coordination. Count each task once. Compare total work with volunteered availability and maintain a separate list of blocked prerequisites. No universal utilization target, hours-online score, compulsory daily submission or reward for overwork.

Before adding work, home manager and host/case appointor check actual compatible windows, relevant competence, conflicts, access and displaced duties. For a protected case, the operating manager can explain/release workload but cannot veto findings, see evidence or choose the reviewer. If willing capacity cannot be released, decline/reduce ordinary services or seek another independent candidate; do not manufacture reviewer availability.

First remove duplicate collection: Heads own their source records, CoS tracks executive commitments, Planning synthesizes allowed aggregates only. One review product has one owner; other roles link it. Allocate routine functional tasks to qualified willing contributors, reduce People Lead delivery, then narrow optional service commitments. Only a remaining demonstrated coaching need can justify another People Lead through existing appointor and capacity rules; never create a layer by ratio.

## Backlog, dependencies and service reduction

Classify unfinished task IDs as ready-for-labor, blocked-by-decision, blocked-by-qualification, blocked-by-authority/access, or intentionally deferred service. A task occupies one class at a time; transitions retain the same ID and remaining effort. A dependency's delay is not another copy of its labor. Spare unrelated hours cannot clear an unqualified or unmandated task. Urgent safety reporting/containment follows existing authority, not a capacity spreadsheet.

Apply B(t)=max(0,B(t-1)+new ready demand-accepted qualified capacity) separately per person and capability; carry backlog forward. When demand exceeds accepted opportunities, named service owner chooses reduced scope/coverage and communicates an honest update. Independent restrictions and appeal eligibility retain their review safeguards; lack of case capacity is not permission for indefinite suspension.

Reassess on changed duties/availability and through existing weekly exception/monthly capacity checks; no new meeting or report. Fixtures and comparative totals are in the [capacity evidence](../capacity-readiness-v1-5.md), not real staffing commitments.
